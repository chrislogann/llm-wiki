# Swastika

**Summary**: An ancient, multicultural symbol of power and the sun that was misappropriated by the Nazi party to represent the struggle for "Aryan victory," resulting in widespread social and legal stigma.

**Sources**: `Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md`, `Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

The swastika is an ancient symbol that is much older than the Nazi party (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). It was historically popular among Germanic, Norse, Anglo-Saxon, and other cultures worldwide, carrying wholesome and ritualistic meanings rather than the radical racial ideologies later associated with it (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).

## Norse Origins and Meanings

- **Thor's Hammer**: Within Norse society, the standard swastika is believed to have signified the god Thor's spinning hammer and the sound of thunder (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).
- **Sonnenrad**: The *sonnenrad* (sunwheel swastika) features curved ends instead of right angles, representing the sun (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). It was found on ancient weapons, temples, and burial sites as a source of inspiration and power (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).

## Entry Into Modern German Politics

The swastika first entered modern German political history during the battles of the [[german-revolution|German Revolution]] (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md). In March 1920, the counter-revolutionary Freikorps troops of Captain Ehrhardt's brigade wore the swastika painted on their helmets during their march on Berlin in the [[kapp-putsch|Kapp Putsch]] (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).

## Nazi Misappropriation

Adolf Hitler defined the swastika in *Mein Kampf* as a symbol representing the mission of "the struggle for the victory of Aryan mankind" (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). The Nazi party's co-optation of the symbol ignored its multicultural origins (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). 
- The *sonnenrad* variant was specifically used on the uniforms of the Waffen-SS Scandinavian divisions 'Wiking' and 'Nordland' (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).
- Due to the strong associations forged by the Nazis, the swastika is legally banned in many institutions and countries today, including Germany under Section 86a of the Criminal Code (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).
