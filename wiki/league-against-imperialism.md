# League Against Imperialism

**Summary**: An international anti-colonial front organization founded by Willy Münzenberg at the 1927 Brussels Congress, designed to align nationalist movements with Soviet foreign policy.

**Sources**: `The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md`, `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The **League Against Imperialism and for National Independence** was established at the Congress of Brussels in February 1927 (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). Organized by [[willi-muenzenberg|Willy Münzenberg]], the organization served as a major [[comintern|Comintern]] front, designed to unite the European communist movement with anti-colonial struggles in Asia, Africa, and Latin America (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## The Brussels Congress (1927)
The founding congress in Brussels brought together 174 delegates representing dozens of colonial countries, alongside prominent Western left-wing intellectuals and pacifists (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). Attendees included:
- **Nationalist Leaders**: Jawaharlal Nehru (future Prime Minister of India), Mohammad Hatta (future Vice President of Indonesia), and Leopold Senghor (future President of Senegal) (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
- **Intellectuals**: Albert Einstein, Romain Rolland, and Upton Sinclair (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

The congress was financed covertly by the [[comintern|Comintern]] via IAH financial channels (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Objectives and Tactics
The League aimed to:
- **Broaden the Base**: Engage non-communist, anti-colonial nationalists who would otherwise refuse direct association with the [[comintern|Comintern]] (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
- **Target Western Powers**: Focus campaigns entirely against British, French, and American imperialism, while avoiding any criticism of Soviet actions (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
- **Coordinate Campaigns**: Sponsor anti-colonial strikes, circulate literature, and coordinate protests in European capitals to support liberation movements in the colonies (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Decline
As Stalin consolidated power, the [[comintern|Comintern]] shifted to a sectarian "Class Against Class" strategy in 1928, demanding that communists break with moderate nationalists (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). Under Moscow's pressure, the League expelled moderate leaders like Nehru, alienating its non-communist base and leading to its decline and ultimate collapse in the 1930s (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
