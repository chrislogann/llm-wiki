# As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns

**Summary**: A report on the resurgence of Soviet-era TV psychic Anatoly Kashpirovsky on YouTube during the 2020 coronavirus pandemic, drawing comparisons to historical figures like Rasputin.

During the 2020 coronavirus pandemic, Soviet-era celebrity psychic Anatoly Kashpirovsky experienced a resurgence in popularity via YouTube, offering online "healing seances" to viewers coping with health anxiety (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).

## Late-Soviet Celebrity and Influence
Kashpirovsky, a former weightlifter and psychologist, achieved massive celebrity status in the Soviet Union starting in 1989 (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md). As the country was collapsing, millions of anxious citizens watched his televised healing sessions to cope with economic decline and systemic changes (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).
* **Mass Popularity**: At his peak in the early 1990s, he packed auditoriums and ranked second only to President Boris Yeltsin in popularity polls (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).
* **Televised Surgical Guide**: In March 1989, Kashpirovsky famously guided a patient, Lesya Yershova, through open abdominal surgery via video link without chemical anesthesia, though she later admitted to feeling terrible pain and only complying to avoid letting him down (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).
* **Rivalry**: Kashpirovsky competed with Allan Chumak, a rival TV psychic who claimed he could "charge" water and skin cream with healing energy through TV screens (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).

## Re-emergence in Times of Crisis
Like historical surges in mysticism (such as Rasputin's influence during the collapse of the Russian Empire), Kashpirovsky's popularity has spiked during major societal crises (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md):
* **2009 Financial Crisis**: Attempted a television comeback with paranormal investigations (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).
* **2020 COVID-19 Pandemic**: Launched online YouTube streams, where his "Coronavirus: Pluses and Minuses" and "Healing Seance" videos attracted hundreds of thousands of views from people seeking immune protection and symptom relief (source: As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md).
