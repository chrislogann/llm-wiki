# Occultism in Nazi Germany

**Summary**: The dual-track exploitation of occultism, astrology, and neo-pagan mysticism by the Nazi regime to establish ideological foundations, manipulate mass psychology, and justify expansionist wars.

**Sources**: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md, Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md, Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-13

---

In the Weimar transition and the Third Reich, the National Socialist regime exploited irrationalism, occult beliefs, and neo-pagan mythology along two parallel tracks: a **state-level neo-pagan mysticism** and a **popular astrological manipulation** (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md, source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

## Dual-Track Occultism

### 1. State-Level Neo-Paganism (Himmler's SS)
Under the leadership of SS chief [[heinrich-himmler]], the regime sought to construct a neo-pagan **"religion of the blood"** to replace traditional Christian institutions (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). 
- **Mystical Influence**: Himmler was a committed occultist who believed in reincarnation and sponsored searches for the "Aryan Holy Grail" (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). He worked closely with [[karl-maria-willigut|Karl Maria Willigut]] ("Himmler's Rasputin"), an occultist claiming Thor's lineage, to fuel mystical nationalism (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).
- **Nordic Misappropriation**: The SS co-opted Norse symbols (like the [[swastika]] and [[sig-rune]]) and myths. Propaganda framed the war against the Soviet Union as the cosmic battle of [[ragnarok]] against the wolf [[fenrir]], casting SS soldiers as elite reincarnations of ancient Viking warriors to recruit volunteers and justify expansion (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).

### 2. Popular Occultism and Mass Manipulation
Concurrently, the Nazi propaganda machine weaponized popular interest in the occult to control public consciousness.
- **Exploiting Superstition**: In *Propaganda als Waffe*, Willi Münzenberg detailed how the party promoted astrology, horoscopes, and clairvoyance to appeal to the ruined middle-class non-voters (*Nichtwähler*), fostering a culture of irrationalism that disabled critical evaluation of Nazi claims (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **The Staged Prophecies of Hanussen**: This track was epitomized by famous clairvoyant [[erik-jan-hanussen]]. Hanussen established close ties to Berlin SA leaders like Helldorff and allegedly coached Hitler in public performance. He "predicted" the [[reichstagsbrand|Reichstag Fire]] days before it occurred due to SA inside leaks, and was subsequently murdered by the SA to cover up the conspiracy (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md, source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).

## Pragmatic Leadership Co-optation
While Himmler was a devoted believer in occult doctrines, other leaders like Joseph Goebbels and Adolf Hitler viewed occultism with pragmatic cynicism. Goebbels dismissed the literal truth of Himmler's neo-paganism but actively promoted both tracks because he recognized their power to bypass rational evaluation, unite Germanic peoples, and direct mass psychology toward the war effort (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md, source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
