# Karl Maria Willigut

**Summary**: An Austrian occultist and SS-Brigadeführer who believed himself descended from the Norse god Thor, known as “Himmler’s Rasputin” for his deep influence on Heinrich Himmler's neo-pagan mysticism and SS ideology.

**Sources**: `Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

**Karl Maria Willigut** (also known by his pseudonym **Karl Maria Wiligut** or his runic code name **Lobesam**) was an Austrian occultist and SS officer who served as one of the primary intellectual architects of the state-level neo-pagan mysticism of the Nazi regime.

## Role as "Himmler's Rasputin"
Willigut claimed to possess ancestral memory and asserted that he was a direct descendant of the Norse god Thor (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). In the early 1930s, he caught the attention of SS leader [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]], who was highly receptive to occultism and mysticism. Himmler recruited Willigut into the SS, where he eventually rose to the rank of SS-Brigadeführer. Because of his pervasive, mystical influence over Himmler’s worldview and the ideological development of the SS, Willigut became widely referred to as **“Himmler’s Rasputin”** (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).

## Constructing the "Religion of the Blood"
Willigut worked in close partnership with Himmler to forge a new German sect centered around a neo-pagan **"religion of the blood"** (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). This ideology was designed to:
- Establish a mythic foundation for German racial superiority and intense nationalism.
- Displace traditional Christian institutions with state-sanctioned neo-pagan rituals and runic symbolism.
- Foster the belief that the specialized military personnel of the SS were elite, fate-driven warriors—essentially reincarnations of ancient Nordic/Viking fighters (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).

## Political Toleration and Propaganda
While other senior Nazi figures, most notably Propaganda Minister Joseph Goebbels, were highly cynical regarding the literal truth of Willigut and Himmler's neo-paganism, they recognized the psychological utility of the myths they constructed. Goebbels actively co-opted Willigut’s imagery and Norse lore for war propaganda, capitalizing on popular interest in the occult to recruit Scandinavian volunteers for the Waffen-SS and to unify the German population behind the war effort (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md).
