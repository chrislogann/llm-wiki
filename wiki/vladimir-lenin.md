# [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]]

**Summary**: The founder of the Bolshevik Party, leader of the 1917 October Revolution, and the first head of state of the Soviet Union, who formulated Marxism-Leninism and established the early Soviet state apparatus.

**Sources**: `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`, `1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md`, `Witness_9780895267894.md`, `My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md`

[[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] (1870–1924), born Vladimir Ilyich Ulyanov, was the principal theoretical and political leader of the Bolshevik wing of the Russian Social Democratic Labor Party (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Following the storming of the Winter Palace in November 1917, he established the early Soviet state (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md).

## Core Ideology and Political Strategies

### 1. Vanguardism
In his 1902 work *What Is to Be Done?*, Lenin introduced vanguardism, arguing that the working class left to itself is only capable of developing "trade union consciousness" (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). He asserted that a successful revolution requires a highly centralized, disciplined party of professional revolutionaries (the Bolsheviks) to lead the proletariat (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

### 2. Cruelty and State Terror
Lenin was a fanatic who tolerated no dissent within his party (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). He repeatedly drove home the message that terror was inevitable and necessary for the survival of the revolution (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Under his administration, the Cheka secret police was established to eliminate class enemies and political rivals (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

### 3. Brest-Litovsk Strategy
When the fledgling Soviet republic faced destruction by the German army in 1918, Lenin, backed by [[joseph-stalin|Stalin]], convinced a reluctant Central Committee to sign the humiliating **Brest-Litovsk Treaty** (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md). The treaty traded vast territories for "breathing room" to consolidate domestic Bolshevik control, demonstrating his tactical flexibility (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md).

### 4. Revolutionary War and the Red Army
Lenin studied Clausewitz’s *On War* and concluded that global peace could only be achieved through "revolutionary war" against international imperialism (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md). He established the Red Army (RKKA) in 1918 and pragmatically enrolled former tsarist military specialists to lead the forces during the [[russian-civil-war|Russian Civil War]] (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md).

### 5. [[peaceful-coexistence|Peaceful Coexistence]]
Coined under Lenin's foreign ministry, the doctrine of **[[peaceful-coexistence|peaceful coexistence]]** was formulated as a tactical truce (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). It allowed the Soviet Union to avoid direct military clashes with capitalist powers while continuing the international class struggle by political, subversive, and proxy means (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

### 6. New Economic Policy (NEP)
Introduced at the Tenth Party Congress in 1921 to replace War Communism, the [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy]] restored market exchanges and a progressive tax in kind to revive the country (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). Although Lenin had initially rejected Trotsky's proposal for a tax in kind in February 1920, he drafted and enacted the guarded NEP theses a year later in response to economic paralysis and uprisings (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).

### 7. Trade Union Debate
In late 1920, Lenin engaged in a sharp controversy with Trotsky over the role of trade unions in the transition to socialism (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). Opposing Trotsky's administrative model of merging unions with state industry, Lenin insisted that trade unions must remain a "school of communism" to train and guide workers rather than coercing them (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
