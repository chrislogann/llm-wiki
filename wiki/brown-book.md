# The Brown Book of the Hitler Terror

**Summary**: A highly influential 1933 counter-propaganda document published by Willy Münzenberg, alongside the London Counter-Trial, which successfully blamed the Nazi regime for the Reichstag fire.

**Sources**: `The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md`, `Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

*The Brown Book of the Hitler Terror and the Burning of the Reichstag* (published in Paris, August 1933) was a major counter-propaganda achievement orchestrated by [[willi-muenzenberg|Willy Münzenberg]] in response to the Nazi consolidation of power and the Reichstag fire (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Context
Following the Reichstag fire on February 27, 1933, Adolf Hitler and his propaganda chief Joseph Goebbels accused the Communist Party (KPD) of plotting a coup, using the event to justify emergency decrees that suspended civil liberties and banned opposition parties (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). Fleeing to Paris, Münzenberg organized an international campaign to counter this narrative (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## The Brown Book
The book was compiled by a team of exiled KPD writers and researchers under Münzenberg’s supervision (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). It presented:
- **Exposures of Nazi Violence**: Documenting details of early concentration camps, torture, and SA terror against Jews, communists, and socialists (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
- **The Reichstag Fire Conspiracy**: Proposing that SA leader Karl Ernst had led a squad through an underground tunnel from Hermann Göring's residence to set the fire, framing the Dutchman Marinus van der Lubbe (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

The book was translated into 17 languages and distributed globally, with copies smuggled back into Germany inside false covers (such as copies of German classic literature) (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

## The London Counter-Trial
To coincide with the book's release and the official German trial in Leipzig, Münzenberg organized an unofficial "Counter-Trial" in London in September 1933 (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). Presided over by a committee of prominent international lawyers and public figures, the counter-trial heard testimony from German exiles and concluded that the Nazis had set the fire themselves, completely exonerating the accused communists (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Historical Assessment
While some details and documents within *The Brown Book* were fabricated or exaggerated by Münzenberg's team (such as the forged diary of SA leader Karl Ernst), the campaign was a masterstroke of [[gegenpropaganda|counter-propaganda]] (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). It successfully seized the narrative initiative, tarnished the international legitimacy of the Third Reich, and established the archetype for modern anti-fascist campaigns (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
