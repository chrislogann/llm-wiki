# Anti-Semitism

**Summary**: Prejudice against, hatred of, or discrimination against Jews as an ethnic, religious, or racial group.

**Sources**: Behemoth_-_the_structure_and_practice_of_national_socialism,_1933-1944_9781566638197.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

In the context of the Third Reich, anti-Semitism was a foundational element of Hitler's ideology. However, historical analyses differ on how to interpret its role. 

From a [[marxist-analysis]] standpoint, such as that presented in [[behemoth]], anti-Semitic actions (like Aryanization) were sometimes seen as opportunistic tools used by the state to appease non-Jewish capitalists or divert public attention from economic failures (source: Behemoth_-_the_structure_and_practice_of_national_socialism,_1933-1944_9781566638197.md). More modern historians typically assert that this interpretation underestimates the true depth of the regime's ideological obsession with race, suggesting that persecution was a primary driver rather than a secondary diversion.
