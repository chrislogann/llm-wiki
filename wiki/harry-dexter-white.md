# [[harry-dexter-white|Harry Dexter White]]

**Summary**: A senior US Treasury Department official who served as a key source for a Soviet underground network in the 1930s, passed confidential Treasury documents to [[whittaker-chambers|Whittaker Chambers]], and co-founded the International Monetary Fund.

**Sources**: `Witness_9780895267894.md`, `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

[[harry-dexter-white|Harry Dexter White]] (1892–1948) was an influential American economist who served as a senior official in the US Department of the Treasury (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). During WWII, he rose to become Assistant Secretary of the Treasury and played a central role in designing the postwar global financial system at the 1944 Bretton Woods Conference, co-founding the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Espionage Activity
During the 1930s, White acted as a highly valuable source for the Soviet GRU intelligence network coordinated by [[whittaker-chambers|Whittaker Chambers]] (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). Rather than a formal, dues-paying Communist Party member, White was a sympathetic fellow traveler who was managed with the assistance of CPUSA organizer George Silverman (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). 

White systematically passed confidential Treasury documents, economic analyses, and handwritten summaries of secret policy discussions to [[whittaker-chambers|Chambers]], who had them photographed for transmission to Moscow (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). 

## Exposure and Death
In August 1948, [[whittaker-chambers|Chambers]] identified White as a source for the Communist underground before the House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC) (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). Elizabeth Bentley, another former Soviet courier, independently named White as a source for her apparatus (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). 

White appeared before HUAC on August 13, 1948, vigorously denying being a Communist or a spy (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). Three days after his testimony, White suffered a severe heart attack and died on August 16, 1948, at the age of 56 (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). 

Subsequent historical evidence, including the declassified Venona Project cables, confirmed White's role as a Soviet agent who operated under the GRU codename "RICHARD" (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
