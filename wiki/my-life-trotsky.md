# My Life - An Attempt at an Autobiography

**Summary**: A summary of Leon Trotsky's autobiography, detailing his role in the Russian Revolution, the Brest-Litovsk peace negotiations, the creation of the Red Army, and his subsequent exile and purge by Stalin.

**Sources**: `My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

Leon Trotsky's *My Life* offers a first-hand account of the major events of the Russian revolutionary movement, the rise and victory of the Bolsheviks, and the subsequent political struggles inside the early Soviet state (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).

## Key Themes

- **Brest-Litovsk Negotiations**: Details his role as Commissar for Foreign Affairs, conducting negotiations with the Central Powers, implementing the "neither war, nor peace" strategy, and using the talks as a platform for revolutionary propaganda (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- **Creation of the Red Army**: Outlines his work in organizing the Red Army from scratch during the Civil War to defend the Soviet state against the White armies and foreign interventionists (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- **Factional Struggles and Exile**: Documents his close collaboration with Lenin and his subsequent political defeat by the rising Soviet bureaucracy led by Joseph Stalin, which co-opted state power, fabricated history, and expelled him from the USSR (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
