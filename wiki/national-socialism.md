# National Socialism

**Summary**: The ideology and practice of the Nazi Party in Germany, often characterized by racism, authoritarianism, and aggressive expansionism.

**Sources**: Behemoth_-_the_structure_and_practice_of_national_socialism,_1933-1944_9781566638197.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

National Socialism, or Nazism, is heavily analyzed regarding its underlying structures and ideologies. In works like [[behemoth]], it is argued that National Socialism lacked a coherent political or social theory, functioning instead as a chaotic coalition of power centers such as the party, state bureaucracy, army, and big business (source: Behemoth_-_the_structure_and_practice_of_national_socialism,_1933-1944_9781566638197.md).

Conversely, other perspectives emphasize that its core derived from an obsession with race, space, and a form of biological materialism, where policies like [[anti-semitism]] were fundamental rather than merely opportunistic.
