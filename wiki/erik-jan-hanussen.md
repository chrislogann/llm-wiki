# Erik Jan Hanussen

**Summary**: An Austrian-Jewish clairvoyant, charlatan, and public performer who established close relationships with leading Nazi figures in Weimar Berlin, allegedly tutored Adolf Hitler in public speaking, and "predicted" the Reichstag Fire before being assassinated by the SA.

**Sources**: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md, Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-13

---

Erik Jan Hanussen (born Herschmann Chaim Steinschneider) was a prominent clairvoyant performer in Weimar Berlin who became deeply entangled with the leadership of the National Socialist German Workers' Party (NSDAP) (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).

## Core Aspects

### Nazi Ties and Tutoring Hitler
Hanussen established secret relationships with leading Nazi officials and SA commanders (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md). According to widespread rumors, Hanussen privately coached Adolf Hitler in public speaking, body language, and performance techniques to maximize his hypnotic impact on crowd psychology (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md). This relationship was later satirized by playwright Bertolt Brecht in *The Resistible Rise of Arturo Ui* (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).

### Exposure by Münzenberg's Press
In 1932, Willi Münzenberg's daily newspaper *Berlin am Morgen* created a major public sensation by publishing a series of exclusive exposés (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md). The reports detailed Hanussen's fraudulent methods, his secret Nazi connections, and the financial schemes he conducted with high-ranking party members (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).

### The Reichstag Fire "Prediction" and Murder
A few days before the [[reichstagsbrand|Reichstag Fire]] on February 27, 1933, Hanussen publicly prophesied during a seance that he "saw a large building burning" (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). This "prophetic vision" was not supernatural, but rather the result of a leak from his close associate Wolf-Heinrich Graf von Helldorff, a prominent Berlin SA leader who was privy to the Nazi plan (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). 

Because Hanussen knew too much about the SA's involvement in the fire, he was kidnapped and murdered in March 1933, likely by a gang of SA men seeking to bury the conspiracy (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md, source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

### Astrology as a Propaganda Tool
In *Propaganda als Waffe*, Münzenberg notes that the Nazi party actively encouraged and exploited public interest in astrology, horoscopes, and clairvoyance to manipulate the ruined middle-class non-voters (*Nichtwähler*), fostering a culture of irrationalism that made them susceptible to fascist mysticism (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). This popular occult track, analyzed in the context of [[occultism-in-nazi-germany]], ran parallel to the state-level neo-paganism and myth-making championed by [[heinrich-himmler]] and his chief mystic Karl Maria Willigut (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). Both tracks were cynically tolerated or co-opted by Joseph Goebbels as powerful tools to unify Germanic peoples and direct mass psychology toward the war effort (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md, source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
