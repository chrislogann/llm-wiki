# Erik Jan Hanussen

**Summary**: An Austrian-Jewish clairvoyant, charlatan, and public performer who established close relationships with leading Nazi figures in Weimar Berlin, allegedly tutored Adolf Hitler in public speaking, and "predicted" the Reichstag Fire before being assassinated by the SA.

**Sources**: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md, Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md, Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

Erik Jan Hanussen (born Herschmann Chaim Steinschneider) was a prominent Austrian-Jewish clairvoyant performer in Weimar Berlin who became deeply entangled with the leadership of the National Socialist German Workers' Party (NSDAP) (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

## Core Aspects

### Background and Identity
Hanussen was born in Vienna into the home of a poor Jewish actor (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). To operate in the nationalistic and increasingly anti-Semitic Austrian and German performance circuits, he fabricated a Danish aristocratic heritage and hid his Jewish ancestry (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

### Nazi Ties and Tutoring Hitler
Hanussen established relationships with leading Nazi officials and SA commanders in Berlin, most notably Wolf-Heinrich Graf von Helldorff (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). Historical evidence, including testimonies from Black Front leader Otto Strasser, indicates that Hanussen met Adolf Hitler in June or July 1932 at the Hotel Kaiserhof, which served as Hitler's Berlin command post (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).
- **Public Speaking Coaching**: Hanussen gave Hitler regular private lessons in public speaking, body language, mass psychology, and the dramatic staging of meetings to maximize his hypnotic impact on crowds (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). This relationship was later satirized by Bertolt Brecht in *The Resistible Rise of Arturo Ui* (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).
- **University of the Occult**: Hanussen proposed establishing a "University of the Occult" to support the ideological anchoring of the future *Führerstaat*, a proposal Hitler supported (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).
- **Newspaper Format Alignment**: Following these meetings, Hanussen redesigned his tabloid, *Bunte Wochenschau* (B/V), formatting it with Fraktur print (the archaic Gothic script favored by Hitler) and shifting the editorial focus toward promoting Hitler's candidacy (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

### Exposure by Münzenberg's Press
In 1932, Willi Münzenberg's newspaper *Berlin am Morgen* created a major public sensation by publishing a series of exposés that detailed Hanussen's Jewish ancestry, fraudulent psychic methods, secret Nazi connections, and financial schemes with high-ranking party members (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).

### The Reichstag Fire "Prediction" and Murder
On February 26, 1933, during a public seance, Hanussen prophesied that he saw a "large government building burning," a day before the [[reichstagsbrand|Reichstag Fire]] occurred (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). Rather than a supernatural vision, this was based on insider leaks from Helldorff, who was privy to the SA plan to commit the arson (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). 
Because Hanussen knew too much about the SA's conspiracy and held substantial financial debts over Helldorff, he was kidnapped and assassinated by a gang of SA men in late March 1933 (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

### Astrology as a Propaganda Tool
In *Propaganda als Waffe*, Münzenberg notes that the Nazi party actively encouraged and exploited public interest in astrology, horoscopes, and clairvoyance to manipulate the ruined middle-class non-voters (*Nichtwähler*), fostering a culture of irrationalism that made them susceptible to fascist mysticism (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). This popular occult track, analyzed in the context of [[occultism-in-nazi-germany]], ran parallel to the state-level neo-paganism and myth-making championed by [[heinrich-himmler]] and his chief mystic Karl Maria Wiligut (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md). Both tracks were cynically tolerated or co-opted by Joseph Goebbels as powerful tools to unify Germanic peoples and direct mass psychology toward the war effort (source: Nazism and Nordic Mythology.md, source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
