# Empire of Justice

**Summary**: The universalist ideology driving Soviet foreign policy and interventionism, emphasizing collective modernity, social justice, state-led economic development, and historical progress toward socialism.

**Sources**: `Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The "Empire of Justice" represents the ideological framework of Soviet foreign relations and Third World interventionism (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). Grounded in Marxist-Leninist theory, Soviet leaders viewed the USSR as a progressive vanguard state destined to assist the global transition from capitalism and imperialism to socialism (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Core Pillars of the Ideology

### 1. Collective Modernity and Social Justice
In contrast to the American emphasis on the individual, the Soviet model prioritized collective rights, social planning, and state-led economic transformation (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). Social justice was defined as the elimination of class exploitation and the equitable redistribution of resources through state control (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 2. State-Led Industrialization
Soviet modernization stressed the rapid construction of heavy industry, infrastructure, and collective agriculture (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). The Soviet model offered Third World countries a roadmap to bypass the gradual, capital-accumulating stages of capitalism and leapfrog directly into industrial modernity (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 3. Scientific Laws of History
Bolshevik ideology maintained that human history is governed by scientific laws that inevitably lead to the collapse of capitalism and the victory of socialism (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). Soviet intervention was therefore framed not as aggression, but as assisting natural historical progress (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Cold War Application
The "Empire of Justice" led Moscow to establish the **[[comintern]]** and later provide military, economic, and educational assistance to national liberation movements (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). In the 1970s, this ideology motivated interventions in Angola, Ethiopia, and Afghanistan to protect fledgling Marxist regimes under the concept of "internationalist duty" and "brotherly assistance" (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
