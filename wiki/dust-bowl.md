# Dust Bowl

**Summary**: An ecological and human disaster during the 1930s characterized by severe dust storms and soil erosion across the Southern Plains of the United States.

**Sources**: [The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md], [Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md]

**Last updated**: 2026-08-15

---

The Dust Bowl was caused by a combination of severe drought, unusually high temperatures, and decades of destructive farming practices (such as deep plowing that eliminated native grasses) (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md). When strong winds swept across the Southern Plains, they created massive dust storms that stripped the topsoil and made agriculture impossible (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).

## Capitalism and Environmental Blunder
Environmental historian [[donald-worster|Donald Worster]] argues that the Dust Bowl was one of the worst ecological blunders in human history, occurring in a span of just 50 years (source: Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md). He traces the root cause of the disaster to the expansionary, maximizing nature of capitalist agriculture, which treated the Southern Plains as capital to be exploited without regard for long-term ecological limits (source: Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md). This drove the rapid sodbusting and plowing of over 100 million acres, setting the stage for the disaster (source: Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md).

## Impact on Tenants and Sharecroppers
The disaster ruined crops and left tenant farmers unable to pay their debts to landowners and banks (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md). Landowners turned to heavy mechanization—"tractoring out" the tenants—to cut costs, resulting in the eviction and displacement of thousands of families (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).

## The Great Migration
Deprived of their livelihoods, approximately 2.5 million people fled the Plains states, with a large percentage migrating west to California in search of agricultural work (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md). This migration is the central backdrop for [[john-steinbeck|John Steinbeck]]'s [[the-grapes-of-wrath]] (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).
