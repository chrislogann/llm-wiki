# Internal Security Forces

**Summary**: The set of paramilitary, intelligence, and law enforcement agencies in Russia separate from the Ministry of Defense, primarily tasked with maintaining domestic order, preventing popular uprisings, and securing the political regime.

**Sources**: `Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

In addition to the regular Armed Forces under the Ministry of Defense, the Russian Federation maintains a massive internal security apparatus, often described as its "other" armed forces or force structures (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md). The size and funding of these agencies reflect the Kremlin's deep preoccupation with domestic stability and regime survival (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).

## Key Force Structures

### 1. Federal Security Service (FSB)
The primary domestic successor to the Soviet KGB, the FSB possesses extensive intelligence-gathering, counter-intelligence, and surveillance powers (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md). The FSB also controls the Border Guard Service and maintains heavily armed elite special operations units (such as Alpha and Vympel) (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).

### 2. Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD)
The MVD oversees the civilian police force and investigates domestic crime (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md). Historically, it also commanded the Internal Troops (VV), a large gendarmerie-style force trained for riot control and internal security operations (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).

### 3. Rosgvardiya (National Guard)
Established by President Vladimir Putin in April 2016 under the command of his former bodyguard Viktor Zolotov, Rosgvardiya is a highly centralized paramilitary force subordinate directly to the President (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md). It absorbed:
- The MVD's Internal Troops (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).
- OMON (Special Purpose Mobile Unit) riot police (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).
- SOBR (Special Rapid Response Unit) tactical units (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).

## Strategic Role: Regime Defense
The internal security forces are essential for maintaining the **[[vertical-of-power]]** (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md). While the regular military is trained to counter external threats, the internal security forces are tasked with protecting the regime against domestic political opposition, civil unrest, and potential foreign-backed social movements ("color revolutions") (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md). Their prominence demonstrates that internal regime preservation is considered a primary military-security threat by the Russian leadership (source: Russia's_military_revival_9781509516148.md).
