# Robert Ley

**Summary**: Robert Ley was the leader of the German Labor Front (DAF) who formulated "after-work socialism" (*Feierabend-Sozialismus*) to reconcile workers to their falling real wages through state-sponsored leisure.

**Sources**: `Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

Robert Ley served as the head of the *Deutsche Arbeitsfront* (DAF), the Nazi state-controlled labor organization that replaced dissolved independent trade unions (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). Under Ley, the DAF utilized aggressive social demagogy to mask the destruction of labor rights and the decline of worker purchasing power (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

## Social Demagogy and Feierabend-Sozialismus

- **Volksgemeinschaft and Redefined Terminology**: Ley promoted the illusion of a unified classless society, renaming employers as "Gefolgschaftsführer" (retinue leaders) and workers as members of a "Gefolgschaft" (retinue) (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). Factories were declared "castles" (*Burgen*) that both parties had a duty to defend (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Feierabend-Sozialismus**: Recognizing the conflict of interest in the workplace, Ley proposed "socialism only after work" (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). In the evening, workers and employers supposedly drank from the same fountain of leisure as equals (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Kraft durch Freude (KdF)**: Ley directed the *Kraft durch Freude* (Strength through Joy) leisure program, which organized cheap state tours and cruise voyages to make workers feel like "Herrenmenschen" (master human beings) without giving them actual economic power or ownership (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Rejection of Wages**: DAF propaganda actively demonized material wage demands as "liberalistic" and "materialist," arguing that work should be performed for the "honor" of serving the nation, similar to a soldier's duty (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

## Economic Reality

Münzenberg highlights the sharp contrast between DAF propaganda and real conditions:
- **Falling Wages**: To fund rearmament, real wages were suppressed (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). A detailed shopping basket comparison of food costs in 1934 vs. 1936 reveals that workers could purchase significantly fewer basic goods (like bread, meat, eggs, and lard) for their money (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Quality Drop**: The actual durability and quality of consumer goods (clothing, tires, soap) declined by approximately 30% (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Corporate Profits**: While workers faced restrictions and handworkers went bankrupt, Germany's top 3,000 millionaires generated over 3 billion marks in profit (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
