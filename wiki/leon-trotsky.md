# Leon Trotsky

**Summary**: Leon Trotsky (1879-1940) was a leading Marxist theorist and Bolshevik revolutionary who led the Brest-Litovsk negotiations and organized the Red Army, before being exiled and purged by Stalin.

**Sources**: `My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md`, `Russia_-_Revolution_and_Civil_War,_1917-1921_9780593493878.md`, `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

Leon Trotsky was one of the central figures of the Russian Revolution, working alongside Vladimir Lenin to establish the early Soviet state and co-founding the [[comintern|Comintern]] in 1919 (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Revolutionary Leadership

Trotsky held key positions during the critical phases of the Soviet state:
- **Commissar for Foreign Affairs**: He headed the negotiations at [[brest-litovsk|Brest-Litovsk]] in 1917-1918, where he implemented the "neither war, nor peace" strategy to broadcast revolutionary ideas to the European working class (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- **Commissar for Military Affairs**: Following Brest-Litovsk, he built and commanded the Red Army from scratch during the [[russian-civil-war|Russian Civil War]], traveling constantly in a specialized armored train to organize resistance and defeat the White forces (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
- **Advocate for World Revolution**: Unlike Joseph Stalin's pragmatic doctrine of "Socialism in One Country," Trotsky insisted on the theory of "Permanent Revolution," asserting that the Russian Revolution could only survive if workers revolted across Western Europe (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Purge, Exile, and Assassination

Following Lenin's death, a bureaucratic faction led by Joseph Stalin seized control of the Communist Party and state apparatus:
- **Purging the Old Guard**: Stalin consolidated power by purging the intellectual "Old Guard" of the revolution, executing former Trotsky allies and Comintern chiefs (including Zinoviev, Kamenev, and Bukharin) during the Moscow show trials (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
- **Fabrication of History**: The Stalinist state systematically rewrote history, removing Trotsky's name and image from historical records, and casting him as the chief conspirator and enemy of the Soviet state (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- **Exile and Fourth International**: Trotsky was expelled from the Communist Party in 1927, banished to Alma-Ata, and exiled from the USSR in 1929; he founded the Fourth International in France in 1938 to sustain orthodox Leninist internationalism (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
- **Assassination (1940)**: In Mexico on August 20, 1940, Trotsky was assassinated with an ice axe by Ramón Mercader, a Spanish communist and NKVD operative acting on direct orders from Stalin (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

This bureaucratic degeneration and the subsequent show trials of Trotskyists are mirrored in the fictional trials of Rubashov in Koestler's *Darkness at Noon* (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
