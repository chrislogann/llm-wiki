# Business System and Fascism

**Summary**: The analysis by economist Robert A. Brady detailing how the modern business system functions as a totalizing, centralized political power that naturally leads to a corporatist, fascist structure.

**Sources**: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

In the late 1930s, economist and economic historian Robert A. Brady argued that the modern business system itself was a totalizing power, making it fundamentally fascist in spirit and intent (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).

## Key Concepts of Brady's Analysis

- **Centralization of Business Policy**: As capitalism evolves, it moves relentlessly toward monopoly, characterized by the centralization of business policy-forming power. Scattered businesses band together into centralized, interest-conscious, and well-funded front organizations to lobby for favorable legislation and exert pressure on the government (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **The Role of Peak Associations**: Brady singled out the **National Association of Manufacturers (NAM)** as one of the most powerful central policy-forming bodies in the United States, alongside retail and banking federations. Similar organizations existed in other advanced industrial nations, all seeking full political, social, and economic coordination to secure class control on behalf of business (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Self-Government in Business**: Business leaders pushed for "self-government" through trade associations and programs like the New Deal's National Recovery Administration (NRA). Brady argued that when "self-government" in business is given legal sanction, it becomes a **corporative system** identical to the corporatism of fascist Italy and Germany, where the state enforces monopoly-capitalist class relations and subjects organized labor (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Authoritarian Philosophy**: The leadership of these central business organizations possessed an authoritarian, undemocratic outlook. They viewed the businessman as the "natural leader" of society and treated the masses as gullible, believing that public opinion could be fully managed using schools, churches, the press, and advertising (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
