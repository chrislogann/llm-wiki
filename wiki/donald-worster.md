# Donald Worster

**Summary**: An influential American environmental historian, Bancroft Prize winner, and author of *Dust Bowl: The Southern Plains in the 1930s*.

**Sources**: [Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md]

**Last updated**: 2026-08-15

---

Donald Worster is a pioneer in the field of environmental history (source: Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md). His scholarship centers on how human cultures and economic systems interact with and reshape the natural world.

## Historical Analysis of the Dust Bowl
In his work on the [[dust-bowl|Dust Bowl]], Worster challenges the traditional view that the crisis was simply an act of nature or a product of unfortunate weather patterns (source: Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md). Instead, he analyzes it as a socio-ecological event, arguing that it was the logical and inevitable outcome of applying a capitalist ethos—which views nature as capital to be aggressively exploited for private wealth—to a highly volatile grasslands environment (source: Dust_Bowl_-_the_southern_Plains_in_the_1930s_9780195174885.md).
