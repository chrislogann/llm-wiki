# Aslan Maskhadov

**Summary**: The Chief of Staff of the Chechen forces during the [[first-chechen-war|First Chechen War]] and later the President of [[chechnya|Chechnya]], known for his military strategy and for signing the Khasavyurt Accord peace agreement.

**Sources**: `Chechnya_9780330350754.md`, `The_New_Tsar_-_the_rise_and_reign_of_Vladimir_Putin_9780307961617.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

Aslan Maskhadov (1951–2005) was a former colonel in the Soviet Red Army artillery who returned to [[chechnya|Chechnya]] in 1992 and was appointed Chief of Staff of the Chechen armed forces under President [[jokhar-dudayev|Jokhar Dudayev]] (source: Chechnya_9780330350754.md). He was widely respected for his professional military organization, moderate political stance, and tactical planning (source: Chechnya_9780330350754.md).

## Military Achievements

### Tactical Defense of Grozny
During the Russian invasion of December 1994, Maskhadov organized the defense of Grozny. He set up mobile tank-hunting teams that successfully ambushed and destroyed the entering Russian armored columns (source: Chechnya_9780330350754.md). When the city fell under relentless bombardment, he executed a disciplined retreat to the mountains to launch a guerrilla campaign (source: Chechnya_9780330350754.md).

### Grozny Counter-Offensive (August 1996)
In August 1996, Maskhadov planned and executed a surprise counter-offensive that infiltrated Grozny, trapping the Russian garrison and forcing Russian Security Council Secretary Alexander Lebed to sign the Khasavyurt Accord (source: Chechnya_9780330350754.md).

## Presidency and the Second War
Maskhadov was elected President of [[chechnya|Chechnya]] in January 1997 in elections monitored by the OSCE, running on a platform of moderate nationalism and reconstruction (source: Chechnya_9780330350754.md). However, his administration was crippled by economic devestation, rivalries with radical field commanders like [[shamil-basayev|Shamil Basayev]], and a wave of kidnappings and lawlessness (source: The_New_Tsar_-_the_rise_and_reign_of_Vladimir_Putin_9780307961617.md).

Following the outbreak of the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]] in 1999, Maskhadov returned to leading the guerrilla resistance in the mountains against Russian forces under [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] (source: The_New_Tsar_-_the_rise_and_reign_of_Vladimir_Putin_9780307961617.md). Despite representing the moderate wing willing to negotiate a peaceful resolution, he was declared a terrorist by Moscow and was killed by Russian special forces during a raid in March 2005 (source: The_New_Tsar_-_the_rise_and_reign_of_Vladimir_Putin_9780307961617.md).
