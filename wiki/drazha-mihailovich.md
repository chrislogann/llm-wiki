# Drazha Mihailovich

**Summary**: The royalist colonel and leader of the Chetnik resistance in WWII Yugoslavia, who fought the German occupation but was discredited by Soviet disinformation, abandoned by the Allies, and executed by Tito's regime.

**Sources**: `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

Drazha Mihailovich (1893–1946) was a colonel in the Royal Yugoslav Army during World War II (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Following the rapid invasion and capitulation of Yugoslavia by Axis forces in April 1941, Mihailovich refused to surrender (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Royalist Resistance (The Chetniks)
On May 10, 1941, Mihailovich hoisted the Yugoslav flag on the mountain of Ravna Gora in Serbia, establishing the royalist "Chetnik" resistance movement to fight the German occupation (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). For the first two years of the war, he was recognized and supported as a major Resistance hero by Great Britain and the United States (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Disinformation Campaign and Abandonment
By late 1943, the Allied command radically altered its stance, accusing Mihailovich of collaborating with the Axis forces and starving his troops of supplies (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Historian [[brian-crozier|Brian Crozier]] highlights that this shift was engineered by Soviet intelligence through a campaign of deliberate disinformation (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). 

A British Communist and NKVD agent, [[james-klugmann|James Klugmann]], who had infiltrated Britain's Special Operations Executive (SOE) in Cairo, systematically intercepted and suppressed reports detailing Mihailovich's successful actions against the Germans while falsifying intelligence to frame him as a collaborator (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Based on this compromised intelligence, Churchill transferred all Allied military support to the Communist Partisans led by [[josip-broz-tito|Josip Broz Tito]] (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Trial and Execution
Isolated and deprived of Allied military aid, Mihailovich was unable to resist both the German forces and Tito's growing army (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Following the Communist takeover of Yugoslavia, Mihailovich was arrested by Tito's forces on March 13, 1946 (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Charged with high treason and war crimes, he was tried in a Soviet-style show trial, sentenced to death, and executed by firing squad in Belgrade on July 17, 1946 (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
