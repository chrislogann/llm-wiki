# Active Measures

**Summary**: A set of offensive political warfare, intelligence, and subversion techniques designed to influence public opinion, compromise political leaders, sow division, and neutralize adversaries.

**Sources**: `Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md`, `The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The term **"active measures"** (*aktivnyye meropriyatiya*) refers to covert and overt political influence operations executed by the Soviet Union (KGB) and its successor agencies in the Russian Federation (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). Unlike standard intelligence collection, active measures are designed to actively manipulate events, compromise opponents, and disrupt target societies (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

## Historical Evolution

### 1. Bolshevik Origins and Comintern
The origins of active measures date back to the founding of the Soviet state (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). Vladimir Lenin authorized early deception operations, instructing officials to maintain a "fictitious separation" between the Soviet state and the **[[comintern]]** (Communist International) to deceive Western powers (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). Under Comintern operatives like **[[willi-muenzenberg]]**, early front organizations were created to manipulate Western public opinion (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

### 2. Shelepin's KGB Master Plan
In 1958, KGB Chairman Alexander Shelepin institutionalized a long-term "strategic disinformation master plan" targeting the United States and NATO (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). The KGB created dedicated departments for *dezinformatsiya* (disinformation) to systematically execute subversion campaigns (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). According to defector Yuri Bezmenov, these subversion campaigns were organized in four stages to incrementally reorient the thinking of the target population (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

## core Techniques

- **Deception (*maskirovka*)**: Hiding or misrepresenting state intentions, military deployments, and covert actions (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).
- **Disinformation (*dezinformatsiya*)**: The propagation of false narratives (such as Operation Infektion in the 1980s claiming the U.S. military created HIV) to undermine public trust (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).
- **Compromising Material (*kompromat*)**: Collecting or fabricating damaging personal information (often sexual or financial) to blackmail and control foreign elites (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).
- **Agents of Influence and "Useful Idiots"**: Cultivating political, academic, or media figures to unwittingly or wittingly amplify narratives that serve the Kremlin's interests (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).
- **Targeted Assassinations ("Wet Affairs" / *mokryye dela*)**: Extralegal assassinations and poisonings targeting political opponents, defectors, and critics (such as the 2019 assassination of Zelimkhan Khangoshvili in Berlin) (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

## Modern Application
Under Vladimir Putin's administration, active measures have been adapted to the internet age (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). Russian state networks (RT, Sputnik), troll factories, and cyber hacking operations are used to exploit domestic social, racial, and political divisions in the United States and Europe, executing covert influence campaigns during elections (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).
