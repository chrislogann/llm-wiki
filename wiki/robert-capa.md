# Robert Capa

**Summary**: A legendary twentieth-century war photographer (1913–1954) who co-collaborated with [[john-steinbeck|John Steinbeck]] on *[[russian-journal|A Russian Journal]]*.

**Sources**: [A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md]

**Last updated**: 2026-08-09

---

Born Endre Ernő Friedmann in Budapest, Hungary, Robert Capa became one of the most celebrated war photojournalists of the twentieth century (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md). Throughout his career, he documented major conflicts, including the [[spanish-civil-war|Spanish Civil War]], the Japanese invasion of China, and World War II, before being killed by a land mine in Indochina in 1954 (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md).

## Collaboration on [[russian-journal|A Russian Journal]]
In 1947, Capa teamed up with [[john-steinbeck|John Steinbeck]] to provide photographic coverage of the Soviet Union (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md). Despite initial Soviet resistance to admitting a foreign photographer, [[john-steinbeck|Steinbeck]] insisted that the journey could only be done as a true collaboration (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md). Capa brought duplicate Contax and Rolleiflex cameras to ensure they could complete their work if any equipment was lost or damaged (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md).

## Portrayal and Personality
In the text of [[russian-journal]], [[john-steinbeck|Steinbeck]] affectionately details Capa's vibrant personality:
* **The Poker Devotee**: Capa is depicted as a disconsolate figure when his poker games end, and he frequently looks for cards and games during their travels (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md).
* **The Charming Traveler**: Capa utilized his charm and language skills to put local hosts at ease, especially in Georgia, and was highly popular with women (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md).
* **The Reluctant Traveler**: [[john-steinbeck|Steinbeck]] frequently jokes about Capa's reluctance to wake up early and his complaints about lack of baths and comfortable beds (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md).
