# Evaluation

**Summary**: Evaluation is the process of checking a designed solution or algorithm against specific criteria to ensure that it is fit for purpose, efficient, complete, and easy to implement.

**Sources**: `What is evaluation - Evaluating solutions - KS3 Computer Science Revision - BBC Bitesize.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

Before converting an algorithm into code, developers must evaluate their planned solution to preemptively fix issues, optimize performance, and ensure it satisfies all project goals (source: What is evaluation - Evaluating solutions - KS3 Computer Science Revision - BBC Bitesize.md).

## Evaluation Criteria

To determine if a proposed algorithm is high quality, it should be evaluated against four primary benchmarks (source: What is evaluation - Evaluating solutions - KS3 Computer Science Revision - BBC Bitesize.md):
- **Clarity**: Is the algorithm easily understood and fully decomposed?
- **Completeness**: Does it solve every aspect of the targeted problem?
- **Efficiency**: Does it solve the problem in the most optimal way, making best use of available resources (e.g., executing as quickly as possible and utilizing the least memory space)?
- **Design Criteria**: Does it meet all predefined standards and constraints outlined in the design phase?

Failing to perform proper evaluation often results in significant difficulties during the programming stage (source: What is evaluation - Evaluating solutions - KS3 Computer Science Revision - BBC Bitesize.md).
