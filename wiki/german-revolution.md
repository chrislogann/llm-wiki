# German Revolution

**Summary**: The German Revolution (1918-1923) was a period of intense civil conflict and political upheaval that ended the German Empire, established the Weimar Republic, and ultimately failed to achieve a workers' state.

**Sources**: `Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md`, `Russia_-_Revolution_and_Civil_War,_1917-1921_9780593493878.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

The German Revolution was a prolonged struggle between reformist social democratic forces and revolutionary workers' movements aiming to establish a council republic (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).

## Key Phases of the Upheaval

1. **The November Revolution (1918)**: Triggered by a sailors' mutiny in Kiel, the revolt quickly spread across the country, leading to the abdication of Kaiser Wilhelm II, the declaration of a Republic, and the formation of workers' and soldiers' councils (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).
2. **The Spartakus Days (January 1919)**: An armed uprising in Berlin led by the newly formed Communist Party (KPD) and the Spartakus League. The Social Democrat government deployed the monarchist Freikorps to crush the uprising, resulting in the executions of Karl Liebknecht and Rosa Luxemburg (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).
3. **The Bavarian Soviet Republic (April 1919)**: A short-lived council republic established in Munich, which was brutally suppressed by government troops and the Freikorps (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).
4. **The [[kapp-putsch|Kapp Putsch]] and the Red Ruhr Army (March 1920)**: Armed workers successfully repelled a right-wing military coup, temporarily establishing workers' power in the Ruhr valley before being crushed by the rebuilt military (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).
5. **The March Action (1921)**: An aborted, premature offensive by the KPD in central Germany that ended in defeat and severe repression (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).
6. **The German October (1923)**: The final failed revolutionary attempt, where plans for a national KPD-led armed insurrection were called off at the last minute, leaving the movement defeated and capitalism stabilized (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).

## Theoretical Legacy

The defeat of the German Revolution had profound global consequences:
- **Isolation of Soviet Russia**: Left the Bolshevik regime isolated in a backward, war-torn economy, laying the groundwork for the rise of Stalinism (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md). This economic isolation was severe following the devastation of the [[russian-civil-war|Russian Civil War]] (1917-1921), which destroyed the country's industrial infrastructure (source: Russia_-_Revolution_and_Civil_War,_1917-1921_9780593493878.md).
- **Rise of Fascism**: Left the old imperial officer corps, judiciary, and big business interests intact, creating the social instability that enabled the rise of Nazism (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md).
