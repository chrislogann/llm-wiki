# SS Totenkopfring

**Summary**: The SS death's head ring, designed by Karl Maria Wiligut and issued by Heinrich Himmler, which served as the premier cult object and symbol of loyalty and neo-pagan solidarity within the SS.

**Sources**: `The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

The **SS Totenkopfring** (death's head ring) was established by Heinrich Himmler as a high-level award for SS officers, serving as a primary physical marker of membership in Himmler’s elite circle (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

## Design and Symbolism

The ring was designed by Himmler's chief occultist, [[karl-maria-willigut]] (Weisthor) (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). The design incorporated specific symbols detailed in the official presentation document:
- **The Death's Head (Totenkopf)**: An admonition to the wearer to be prepared at any time to risk their individual life for the collective whole of the SS (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Two Sig Runes**: Placed opposite the death's head, representing the name of the Schutzstaffel (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **The Swastika and Hagall Rune**: Designed to keep focus on the wearer's unshakable faith in the victory of the National Socialist philosophy (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Oak Leaves**: Crowning the ring, representing the traditional German tree (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

## Regulations and Disposition

Strict rules governed the possession and return of the Totenkopfring:
1. **Non-Transferability**: The ring could not be sold, copied, or transferred to any other individual (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
2. **Return to the Reichsführer-SS**: If an officer withdrew from the SS or died, the ring had to be returned (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
3. **Storage in the Walhalla Vault**: The rings of officers killed in battle were returned to Himmler and stored in a designated repository in the Walhalla vault of [[wewelsburg-castle]] to symbolize their ongoing bond with the order (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). Rings returned under other circumstances (such as retirement or dismissal) were generally melted down (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

At the end of World War II, following the detonation of Wewelsburg Castle on Himmler's orders, the stored cache of hundreds of Totenkopfrings was looted by local residents and advancing American soldiers (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
