# John Steinbeck


**Summary**: American author known for his realistic depictions of the lives of the working class and the Great Depression.


**Sources**: [The Grapes of Wrath 9780143039433.md](llm-wiki/raw/The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md)


**Last updated**: 2026-08-09


---


John Steinbeck (1902–1968) was an American novelist, social activist, and writer. His work often focused on the struggles of the migrant labouring class in California.


His most famous works, such as *The Grapes of Wrath* and *Of Mice and Men*, are celebrated for their empathetic portrayal of human dignity amidst economic hardship.
