# Global Cold War

**Summary**: Odd Arne Westad's historical analysis arguing that the global expansion of the Cold War was driven by the universalist, interventionist ideologies of the United States and the Soviet Union, which reshaped the Third World and laid the groundwork for contemporary global conflicts.

**Sources**: `Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

*The Global Cold War: Third World Interventions and the Making of Our Times* (published in 2005 by Odd Arne Westad) details how the geopolitical rivalry between the United States and the Soviet Union was exported to the developing world (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). Westad asserts that the superpower interventions in Asia, Africa, and Latin America were not merely opportunistic, but were deeply rooted in their respective national ideologies (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Key Themes

### 1. Ideological Universality and Modernity
Both superpowers viewed themselves as the rightful heirs to European modernity and sought to prove the global applicability of their models (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). 
- The United States operated under the **[[empire-of-liberty]]** ideology, emphasizing individual liberty, private property, and free markets (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- The Soviet Union operated under the **[[empire-of-justice]]** ideology, advocating collective modernity, state-led industrialization, and social justice (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 2. The Third World Project
The concept of the "Third World" emerged in the 1950s as newly independent nations sought to construct a political identity separate from the two competing blocs, highlighted by the **[[nonaligned-movement]]** and the Bandung Conference (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). However, local elites frequently aligned with a superpower to obtain resources for nation-building, inadvertently importing the Cold War conflict into their domestic politics (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 3. Proxy Conflicts and Interventions
The peak of global Cold War confrontation occurred in the 1970s and early 1980s, characterized by highly destructive civil wars and foreign interventions (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md):
- **Angola**: The Portuguese decolonization led to the **[[angolan-civil-war]]** where Soviet-backed Cuban forces clashed with US and South African-backed factions (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- **Ethiopia**: The **[[ethiopian-revolution]]** established a Marxist regime under Mengistu Haile Mariam, leading to a Soviet-Ethiopian alliance and the **[[ogaden-war]]** against Somalia (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- **Afghanistan**: The Soviet Union intervened in the **[[soviet-afghan-war]]** to support a modernizing Marxist client state against the **[[reagan-doctrine]]** offensive and rising Islamist resistance (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 4. Collapse and Legacy
Westad argues that these interventions eventually exhausted both superpowers (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). While they contributed to the internal collapse of the Soviet Union, they also left behind highly militarized, unstable states, fueling the rise of anti-Western sentiments and political Islam in the post-Cold War era (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
