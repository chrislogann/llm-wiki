# [[comintern|Comintern]]

**Summary**: The Communist International (Third International), founded by [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] in 1919 to direct and coordinate the worldwide communist revolution, functioning as an instrument of Soviet foreign policy, covert subversion, and international control.

**Sources**: `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`, `Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md`, `Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md`, `The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md`, `Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The Communist International ([[comintern|Comintern]]) was established in Moscow in March 1919 by [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] and [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] following the collapse of the Second International during World War I (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md, source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md). Headquartered in Moscow and governed by the 21 Conditions of Admission, member communist parties were strictly subordinated to the directives of the Executive Committee of the Communist International (ECCI) and the Soviet leadership (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). To deceive Western powers, Lenin directed a "fictitious separation" of the Soviet government from the Comintern, establishing a precedent of strategic deception that would define Soviet **[[active-measures]]** (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

## Operational Evolution

1. **Early Revolutionary Offensive (1919–1923)**:
   - The [[comintern|Comintern]] actively supported and directed revolutionary insurrections in Central Europe, notably during the [[german-revolution|German Revolution]] and the abortive "German October" of 1923 (source: Lost_Revolution_-_Germany_1918_To_1923_9781608463169.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
   - In Asia, the [[comintern|Comintern]] dispatched agents (such as Henk Sneevliet / Maring) to establish links with Sun Yat-sen's Kuomintang and oversee the founding of the Chinese Communist Party in 1921 (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

2. **Propaganda and Front Organizations**:
   - Under the direction of [[willi-muenzenberg|Willi Münzenberg]], the [[comintern|Comintern]] pioneered sophisticated global front organizations (known colloquially as "Münzenberg enterprises" or "innocents' clubs"), including the International Workers' Aid (IAH) and the World League Against Imperialism, to mobilize non-communist Western intellectuals and workers (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

3. **Stalinization and Purges (1928–1939)**:
   - During the 1930s, the [[comintern|Comintern]] was thoroughly subordinated to [[joseph-stalin|Joseph Stalin]]'s domestic political priorities and the doctrine of "Socialism in One Country" (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
   - The Great Terror swept through the [[comintern|Comintern]] apparatus, leading to the liquidation of prominent foreign communist leaders and the execution of early Soviet [[comintern|Comintern]] figures such as Grigory Zinoviev, Nikolai Bukharin, and Karl Radek (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md, source: Darkness_at_Noon_9780226449616.md).
   - During the [[spanish-civil-war|Spanish Civil War]], the [[comintern|Comintern]] organized the International Brigades while Soviet NKVD and [[comintern|Comintern]] operatives exercised political policing over the Republican coalition (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md, source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

4. **Strategic Dissolution and Successor Structures (1943–1991)**:
   - In May 1943, Stalin officially dissolved the [[comintern|Comintern]] as a diplomatic maneuver to reassure his wartime Western allies (the United States and Britain) that Moscow had abandoned global revolution (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
   - The functions of the [[comintern|Comintern]] were preserved through the Communist Information Bureau (Cominform, 1947–1956) and subsequently institutionalized within the International Department of the Central Committee of the CPSU, which continued covert funding and direction of foreign parties until 1991 (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
