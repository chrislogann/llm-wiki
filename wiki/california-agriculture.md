# California Agriculture


**Summary**: The agricultural systems and economic landscape of the California Central Valley, often a focal point for labour movements.


**Sources**: [The Grapes of Wrath 9780143039433.md](llm-wiki/raw/The_Grapes_of_Wrath_97_80143039433.md) (Note: linking to source in relation to literature context)


**Last updated**: 2026-08-09


---


The agricultural industry in California, particularly in the Central Valley and coastal regions, has historically been a site of intense labour struggles. The socio-economic dynamics of this region were central to the migrations described in period literature like *The Grapes of Wrath*.


The availability of seasonal work and the impact of environmental factors like drought have shaped the state's economic history.
