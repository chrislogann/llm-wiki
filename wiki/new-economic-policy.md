# [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy]]

**Summary**: An economic policy introduced by the Soviet government in 1921 that replaced War Communism, restoring a degree of private trade and market mechanisms to revive the devastated national economy.

**Sources**: `My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The **[[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy]]** (NEP) was adopted by the Bolshevik government at the Tenth Party Congress in March 1921 (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). It marked a strategic retreat from the extreme centralization of War Communism, reintroducing market exchanges, private ownership of small businesses, and a monetary economy to stabilize the country after three years of civil war (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).

## Trotsky's Early 1920 Proposal
According to Leon Trotsky's account in *My Life*, the transition to the NEP could have happened a year earlier (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). In February 1920, drawing on observations from his economic work in the Urals, Trotsky submitted a proposal to the Central Committee advocating that War Communism be abandoned (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md):
- He argued that the policy of equalized grain requisition (prodrazvyorstka) lowered the status of agriculture, dispersed the industrial proletariat, and threatened a complete economic breakdown (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- He proposed replacing the grain levy with a progressive tax in kind (prodnalog) structured to make increasing ploughed areas profitable, and establishing a home market for the exchange of commodities (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- However, [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] firmly opposed the proposal, and the Central Committee rejected it by a vote of 11 to 4 (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). The party instead spent another year pursuing War Communism.

## The Blind Alley and Trade Union Dispute
The rejection of the market system led to what Trotsky describes as an economic "blind alley" (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md):
- **War Methods**: With market relations banned, Trotsky demanded that the militarized administrative methods of War Communism be applied systematically to get results, particularly in transport where he successfully standardized repairs and saved the railways from collapse using strict discipline (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- **Trade Union Dispute**: Because the state controlled all distribution under War Communism, Trotsky argued that trade unions should be incorporated directly into the state administration of industry (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). This sparked the bitter "trade union discussion" in late 1920, which Stalin and Zinoviev used to campaign against Trotskyism (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- Lenin, sensing the political crisis and the exhaustion of the working class, moved to ease military pressure, leading to their temporary political disagreement on the eve of the transition (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).

## Introduction of the Policy
The Kronstadt rebellion and peasant uprisings in Tambov in early 1921 served as final warnings, prompting Lenin to draft the first guarded theses of the New Economic Policy (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md). 
- Trotsky immediately supported the theses, as they aligned with his February 1920 proposals (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
- Once the NEP was implemented, the trade union dispute lost all significance, and Lenin subsequently formulated entirely new principles on the role of trade unions in a market-oriented state economy (source: My_Life_-_An_Attempt_at_an_Autobiography_9780486123400.md).
