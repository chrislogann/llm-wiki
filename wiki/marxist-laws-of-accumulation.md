# Marxist Laws of Accumulation

**Summary**: The economic laws governing the expansion of capital, the rising organic composition of capital, the displacement of labor, and the systemic crises of overproduction that lay the groundwork for the dictatorship of capital.

**Sources**: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md, The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-13

---

In Marxist political economy, the **general law of capitalist accumulation** describes the internal dynamics and contradictions that drive the development of capitalist societies. In the context of Michael Joseph Roberto's *The Coming of the American Behemoth*, these laws explain how the economic growth of the "Great Boom" in the 1920s inevitably led to the Great Depression and created the structural conditions for the [[dictatorship-of-capital]] (fascism) (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).

## Core Mechanisms

- **Organic Composition of Capital**: Capital is divided into constant capital ($C$, representing machinery, technology, and raw materials) and variable capital ($V$, representing human labor-power). The drive to increase productivity leads capitalists to invest heavily in machinery, raising the ratio of $C$ to $V$ (the organic composition of capital) (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Displacement of Labor**: As the organic composition of capital rises, living labor is increasingly replaced by machines. This process creates a permanent, disposable **industrial reserve army** of the unemployed and underemployed, which capitalists use to check rising wages and keep labor subjugated (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Concentration and Centralization**: Intense competition drives less efficient capital out of the market. This leads to the centralization of wealth into monopolies and corporate behemoths, backed by the expansion of the credit system (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).

## Historical Application (The 1920s and 1930s U.S.)

In *The Decline of American Capitalism* (1934), Lewis Corey applied Marx's laws of accumulation to analyze the 1920s U.S. economy:
- Between 1923 and 1929, the U.S. saw a massive buildup of constant capital, which grew more than four times faster than variable capital. Workers set in motion one-third more machinery and produced one-fifth more output, but their wages remained stagnant (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- This disparity led to a crisis of overproduction and underconsumption. Lacking profitable outlets in productive industries, capital was diverted into speculative ventures, directly precipitating the Wall Street crash of 1929 and the Great Depression (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).

## Literary Depiction (The Grapes of Wrath)

John Steinbeck's [[the-grapes-of-wrath]] serves as a narrative depiction of these economic laws:
- **Tractorization as Rise in $C$**: The eviction of tenant farmers by tractors represents the rise in the organic composition of capital ($C$) replacing human labor ($V$) (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).
- **The Migrant Pool as the Industrial Reserve Army**: The influx of over 300,000 dispossessed migrants to California forms an "industrial reserve army" that allows large landowners to lower wages below subsistence levels (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).
- **Marx as a Historical Result**: Steinbeck frames revolutionary ideas and theorists (like Marx and Lenin) not as arbitrary causes of unrest, but as natural results of structural inequality and concentration of ownership (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).

## Connection to Fascism

Fascism is seen as a direct property of capital's attempt to resolve these structural contradictions of accumulation. When the crisis of overproduction threatens profits and stability, the capitalist class consolidates its rule over society. This is achieved through both coercive state coordination and "non-terrorist" coordination like advertising and marketing to manipulate public consciousness and sustain consumption (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
