## Concepts

- [[heinrich-himmler]] - The occultism of the SS leader, the "religion of blood," and the integration of terror.
- [[swastika]] - Ancient Norse representation of the sun and Thor's hammer vs. Nazi co-optation and subsequent legal bans.
- [[sig-rune]] - Runic letter "S" redesigned into the SS *siegrune* (victory rune).
- [[viking-expansion]] - The historical reality of decentralized Viking expansion vs. the Nazi propaganda of a predestined empire.
- [[fenrir]] - The mythological wolf Fenrir and its co-optation to represent the Soviet threat.
- [[ragnarok]] - Norse judgment day and its utilization to suggest a Northern alliance.
- [[berserks]] - God-fueled Norse warriors and how their imagery was co-opted to frame Nazi collaboration as righteous.
- [[willi-muenzenberg]] - Anti-Nazi communist media publisher and pioneer of counter-propaganda.
- [[mein-kampf]] - Hitler's manifesto detailing the emotional, anti-intellectual, and deceptive rules of Nazi propaganda.
- [[robert-ley]] - Leader of the DAF who instituted "after-work socialism" while real worker wages fell.
- [[reichstagsbrand]] - The 1933 fire used by Goebbels and Göring as a master lie to seize total power.
- [[gegenpropaganda]] - The theory and practice of counter-propaganda as an offensive weapon against fascist regimes.
- [[nicolas-rubashov]] - Fictional old Bolshevik protagonist of *Darkness at Noon*, his moral compromises, and execution.
- [[gletkin]] - The "New Guard" interrogator who uses physical and sleep deprivation to extract confessions.
- [[grammatical-fiction]] - The concept of the first-person singular ("I"), conscience, and guilt over Arlova.
- [[relative-maturity-of-the-masses]] - The theory that dictatorship is a necessity during transitions when the masses are politically immature.
- [[spanish-civil-war]] - The conflict (1936-1939) between the Second Republic and Nationalist rebel forces under General Francisco Franco.
- [[german-revolution]] - The period of civil war and upheaval in Germany (1918-1923) that established the Weimar Republic.
- [[kapp-putsch]] - The right-wing military coup attempt in March 1920 defeated by a national general strike.
- [[chinese-eastern-railroad]] - The strategically vital railway in Manchuria that was the flashpoint of the 1929 war.
- [[roman-von-ungern-sternberg]] - Baron Roman von Ungern-Sternberg (the "Mad Baron"), his capture of Urga, and subsequent defeat.
- [[russian-civil-war]] - The multi-party conflict (1917-1921) that ended the Russian Empire and established the USSR.
- [[brest-litovsk]] - The treaty (March 1918) ending Russia's role in World War I, and a major diplomatic battleground.
- [[leon-trotsky]] - Bolshevik revolutionary, commander of the Red Army, and author of *My Life*.
- [[soviet-censorship]] - State control of foreign reporting, media vetting, and film confiscation in the USSR.
