# James Klugmann

**Summary**: A British Communist and NKVD agent within the Cambridge Spy Ring who infiltrated the British Special Operations Executive (SOE) during WWII, orchestrating a disinformation campaign that led the Allies to abandon [[drazha-mihailovich|Drazha Mihailovich]] and support [[josip-broz-tito|Tito]].

**Sources**: `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

James Klugmann (1912–1977) was a prominent British Communist intellectual who was recruited by Soviet intelligence (NKVD/KGB) in the 1930s as part of the **Cambridge Spy Ring** (which included Kim Philby, Guy Burgess, Donald Maclean, and Anthony Blunt) (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). Fellow agents described Klugmann as the most brilliant member of the group (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Infiltration of the SOE in Cairo
During World War II, Klugmann joined the British Army and succeeded in infiltrating the Yugoslav Section of the **Special Operations Executive (SOE)** based in Cairo, which was responsible for coordinating British intelligence and military aid to resistance groups in the Balkans (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## The Disinformation Campaign
In his role at the SOE, Klugmann executed a highly successful campaign of disinformation on behalf of the Soviet [[comintern|Comintern]] to secure a Communist takeover in postwar Yugoslavia (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md):
- **Suppression of Intelligence**: Klugmann systematically intercepted and suppressed official dispatches sent by British liaison officers in the field that reported on royalist leader [[drazha-mihailovich|Drazha Mihailovich]]'s military operations against the Germans (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
- **Falsification of Reports**: He altered incoming reports and fabricated intelligence to portray Mihailovich's forces as Axis collaborators, while exaggerating the success and military size of [[josip-broz-tito|Josip Broz Tito]]'s Communist Partisans (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

Based on the falsified reports generated by Klugmann's Cairo office, Winston Churchill withdrew British support and supplies from Mihailovich and diverted all Allied aid to Tito (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). This intelligence failure paved the way for the elimination of the royalist opposition and the postwar establishment of Tito's Communist dictatorship (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).

## Later Career
After the war, Klugmann returned to the Communist Party of Great Britain (CPGB), serving as its official historian and editor of its theoretical journal *Marxism Today* until his death in 1977 (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md).
