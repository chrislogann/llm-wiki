# Internationale Arbeiterhilfe

**Summary**: The International Workers' Relief (IAH) was a [[comintern|Comintern]] front organization founded by Willy Münzenberg in 1921 that raised famine relief for Soviet Russia while acting as a commercial enterprise and propaganda network.

**Sources**: `The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md`, `Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The **Internationale Arbeiterhilfe** (IAH, known in English as International Workers' Relief) was established in August 1921 by [[willi-muenzenberg|Willy Münzenberg]] in response to [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]]’s appeal for international aid to counter the catastrophic Volga famine in Soviet Russia (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Famine Relief and Soft Power
The initial goal of the IAH was to collect funds and supplies from Western workers, trade unions, and sympathetic intellectuals (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). By positioning itself as a neutral, humanitarian relief agency rather than an official KPD or [[comintern|Comintern]] organization, the IAH successfully bypassed anti-communist restrictions and appealed to a broad base of progressive liberals (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Commercial Operations
Under Münzenberg’s direction, the IAH expanded from a temporary relief agency into a permanent, multi-million dollar business empire (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md). Key ventures included:
- **Tractor Imports**: Purchasing and importing hundreds of American tractors into Russia to assist with agricultural mechanization, establishing model farms (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
- **Industrial Concessions**: Operating fisheries, workshops, and commercial enterprises within the Soviet Union (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
- **Media and Entertainment**: Running cinemas, theatres, and book publishers in Germany and Russia (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).

## Social Initiatives
In Germany, the IAH organized relief stations, soup kitchens, and children’s homes during the economic crises and hyperinflation of 1923 (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). This concrete social assistance won the loyalty of many working-class families and was later plagiarized by the National Socialist regime to create their own state-run charity program, the *Winterhilfswerk* (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

The IAH was dissolved in 1935 as the [[comintern|Comintern]] shifted its focus toward building formal Popular Front coalitions (source: The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md).
