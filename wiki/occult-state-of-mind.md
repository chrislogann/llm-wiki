# Occult State of Mind

**Summary**: An exploration of the psychological, systemic, and evolutionary drivers that lead political elites, citizens, and ideologues to turn to occultism, mysticism, and self-deception during times of perceived crisis.

**Sources**: `The_New_Tsar_-_the_rise_and_reign_of_Vladimir_Putin_9780307961617.md`, `Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md`, `1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md`, `Darkness_at_Noon_9780226449616.md`, `Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md`, `In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md`, `As COVID-19 Hits Russia, A Self-Styled Psychic Healer And Soviet-Era Icon Returns.md`, `5-Voloshyna.md`, `A “Common Madness” TV Psychics and Hypnosis in the Soviet Union.md`, `Soviet Union’s mass delusion Psychic healer who ‘cured’ millions through TV dies.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-18

---

The recourse to occultism, psychic beliefs, and irrationalism is rarely an arbitrary choice; rather, it is a patterned psychological response to extreme systemic stress. When rational frameworks (such as economics, law, or medicine) collapse, both individual leaders and entire populations frequently retreat into non-rational frameworks to preserve their sanity, explain their failures, and reclaim a sense of control.

## Core Psychological and Systemic Drivers

### 1. Ego Preservation and Cognitive Dissonance
When an individual's or group's reality conflicts violently with their self-image, the mind experiences severe cognitive dissonance. Occult explanations act as a protective shield:
* **The Individual Level ([[anatoly-sobchak|Anatoly Sobchak]]):** During a critical 1996 televised debate, St. Petersburg Mayor Anatoly Sobchak suffered sudden throat spasms, a thick tongue, and a fever, causing a humiliating defeat. Unable to reconcile his identity as a brilliant law professor and orator with this public failure, Sobchak claimed he was targeted by a psychic in the audience. Attributing his collapse to "alien energy" allowed him to preserve his ego.
* **The Collective Level (Weimar German Citizens):** Following the humiliation of WWI and the subsequent economic collapse, German citizens could not reconcile their self-image as a highly advanced cultural nation with their sudden poverty. Adopting myths of Nordic and Aryan superiority ([[occultism-in-nazi-germany|occultism in Nazi Germany]]) resolved this dissonance, restoring collective self-worth.

### 2. Collapse of Rational Predictability
Under stable conditions, individuals rely on cause-and-effect relationships (e.g., hard work leads to financial stability; logical arguments win debates; medical institutions cure diseases). When systemic crises shatter these rules, the brain defaults to hyper-active pattern recognition:
* **The Economic Void:** Facing Weimar hyperinflation and the Great Depression, where traditional work yielded no stability, citizens turned to astrologers and clairvoyants (like [[erik-jan-hanussen]]) to discover hidden, cosmic order.
* **The Political Chaos:** Navigating the unstable transition of post-Soviet politics and facing corruption investigations, Sobchak abandoned rational political analysis and turned to pseudo-scientific "experts" to diagnose his debate spasms.
* **Soviet Collapse (1989-1991) and Healthcare Collapse**: During the chaotic transition and economic collapse of the late-Soviet Union (accelerated by the Chernobyl disaster), millions of citizens turned to televised healers like [[anatoly-kashpirovsky]] and [[allan-chumak]]. They sought order, healing, and control through TV screens, blending scientific medical jargon with vitalist concepts of self-healing (source: 5-Voloshyna.md). This was heavily driven by the near-total collapse of the public healthcare system; facing pharmaceutical shortages, long queues, and corruption, turning to TV psychics became a highly pragmatic choice for citizens lacking alternatives (source: A “Common Madness” TV Psychics and Hypnosis in the Soviet Union.md).
* **Late-Soviet Spiritual Confusion**: The fatal discredit of communist doctrine left a "spiritual confusion" in the late-1980s, leaving the public highly suggestible to white magic, Scientology, and astrology (source: A “Common Madness” TV Psychics and Hypnosis in the Soviet Union.md). This suggestibility was famously demonstrated in 1991 when musician Sergei Kurekhin broadcast a television prank asserting that Lenin's genetic makeup had transformed into a mushroom due to consuming psychoactive mushrooms, prompting panicked viewers to call broadcasters to verify the story (source: A “Common Madness” TV Psychics and Hypnosis in the Soviet Union.md).
* **Wartime and Economic Crisis (2026):** Faced with military threats (the ongoing offensive in Ukraine) and economic instability (rising cost of living), contemporary Russians have increasingly abandoned rational frameworks, turning to occult practitioners and buying crystal balls or aspen stakes as a form of "psychological defense" (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).

### 3. Reclaiming Agency in Helplessness
Occult beliefs and rituals function as surrogate actions in situations of absolute helplessness:
* **The Boxers' Supernatural Armor:** Facing desperate poverty and foreign imperialist partition in 1900, Chinese Boxers (*Yi Ho Chuan*) turned to martial rituals claiming the **"armor of the golden bell"**—a psychic invulnerability to bullets. Though militarily helpless, the belief gave them the psychological agency to fight (source: [[sino-soviet-war-1929|1929 Sino-Soviet War]]).
* **The "Mad Baron" [[roman-von-ungern-sternberg|Roman von Ungern-Sternberg]]:** Shattered by the collapse of the Russian Empire and the Bolshevik revolution, the Baron integrated Mongolian prophecies and Tibetan Buddhism. He sincerely believed he was a reincarnated god of war, transforming the chaos of the Russian Civil War into an ordered, apocalyptic crusade.
* **Spells for Soldiers on the Front:** Under the constant threat of death and separation, soldiers in eastern Ukraine contact witches for protection from evil and love spells to ensure their partners' fidelity, using rituals to cope with conditions where they have no direct agency (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).
* **The Pandemic Threat (2020-2021):** Faced with the novel threat of COVID-19 and the failures of the state and mainstream medical systems, citizens turned to Kashpirovsky's YouTube live streams (source: 5-Voloshyna.md). According to David Hufford's *experiential source hypothesis*, these sessions provided a surrogate sense of agency and immunoprotection for marginalized groups, such as Russian-speaking immigrants in Brighton Beach who lacked health insurance (source: 5-Voloshyna.md).

## The Totalitarian Counter-Perspective: Logic vs. Mystical Ecstasy

Totalitarian systems view the turn toward the irrational as a threat to state discipline, yet they also recognize its power.

### Ivanov on "Cheap Ecstasies"
In [[darkness-at-noon|Darkness at Noon]], the interrogator Ivanov argues that the recourse to guilt, conscience, and mysticism is a dangerous, self-indulgent retreat from reality:
> *"Every bottle of spirits contains a measurable amount of ecstasy. Unfortunately, only few people, particularly amongst our fellow countrymen, ever realize that the ecstasies of humility and suffering are as cheap as those induced chemically."*

To Ivanov, Rubashov’s awakening conscience (his **[[grammatical-fiction|grammatical fiction]]**) is a form of emotional escapism. Ivanov demands a **"vivisection morality"**—treating human units mathematically—and warns that any compromise with one's conscience is a betrayal of the cause.

### Rubashov’s Retreat into the "Oceanic Sense"
Despite the Party's warnings against "petit-bourgeois mysticism," Rubashov, facing execution, surrenders to the **"oceanic sense"**—a feeling of dissolving into the infinite where logical boundaries disappear. In his final hours, he recognizes that the Party's pure mathematical reason ran amuck and failed: *"No, the equation did not work out... one cannot build Paradise with concrete."*
