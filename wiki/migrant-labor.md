# Migrant Labor

**Summary**: The social and economic framework of transient agricultural workers who migrated to California during the [[great-depression|Great Depression]].

**Sources**: [The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md]

**Last updated**: 2026-08-09

---

During the 1930s, the influx of hundreds of thousands of displaced farmers from the [[dust-bowl|Dust Bowl]] created a massive surplus of agricultural workers in California (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md). This surplus was exploited by corporate farm owners to suppress wages and prevent collective bargaining (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).

## Living Conditions
Migrant laborers lived in squalid, unsanitary roadside camps, ditch-bank settlements, or company-owned shacks (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md). Families suffered from malnutrition, lack of medical care, and continuous hostility from local residents and law enforcement who viewed them as a burden and a threat (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).

## Labor Organizing and Conflict
As wages plummeted below subsistence levels, spontaneous strikes and unionization efforts broke out, only to be met with violence and intimidation by grower associations and local deputies (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md). This struggle for dignity and survival is depicted through the experiences of characters in [[the-grapes-of-wrath]] (source: The_Grapes_of_Wrath_9780143039433.md).
