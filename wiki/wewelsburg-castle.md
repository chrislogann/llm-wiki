# Wewelsburg Castle

**Summary**: A 17th-century castle in Westphalia, Germany, transformed under the direction of Heinrich Himmler and Karl Maria Wiligut into the global headquarters, ideological center, and primary ritual space of the SS.

**Sources**: `The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

**Wewelsburg Castle**, located near Büren in Westphalia, was acquired by the SS in 1933 and transformed into an ideological "Order Castle" (*Ordensburg*) and high-level training school for SS leaders (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

## Concept and Development

Heinrich Himmler first viewed the castle in 1933 during a campaign trip. Greatly influenced by the occultist [[karl-maria-willigut]], Himmler conceptualized the castle as a worldwide headquarters for a new pagan order of knights—the SS—and as the metaphysical "center of the world" (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). 

It was managed under the *Gesellschaft zur Förderung und Pflege deutscher Kulturdenkmäler* (Society for the Promotion and Care of German Cultural Monuments) and remodeled extensively to host rituals specific to Himmler’s elite circle (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

## Ritual Spaces of the North Tower

The focus of the SS cult at Wewelsburg was the northern tower, which was remodeled with distinct architectural elements:

### 1. The Walhalla (Hall of the Slain)
Located in the lowest vault of the northern tower, the Walhalla served as a solemn chamber dedicated to deceased SS members (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). 
- **Design**: The domed ceiling features a central swastika with ventilation/acoustic holes that provide unique acoustics in the space (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Function**: The vault was intended to house the [[ss-totenkopfring|death's head rings]] of all SS officers who fell in battle, serving as a symbolic resting place for their spirits (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

### 2. The Colonnade Chamber
Situated directly above the Walhalla vault, this colonnade hall was designed as the central ritual chamber for the SS order of knights (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Floor Emblem**: The center of the room is decorated with a marble floor emblem depicting a radial design composed of twelve [[sig-rune|Sig runes]] radiating outwards (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

## Destruction and Looting

As American troops approached Westphalia in March 1945, Himmler ordered the destruction of the castle to prevent its capture (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). SS men detonated explosives on March 31, 1945, leaving the structure heavily damaged (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). In the days before American troops secured the site, local villagers looted the ruins, and subsequent arriving American forces collected remaining artifacts, including hundreds of stored death's head rings (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
