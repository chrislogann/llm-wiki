# Gegenpropaganda

**Summary**: The strategy of counter-propaganda advocated by Willi Münzenberg to combat fascist lies and terror through truth, humor, media saturation, and active resistance.

**Sources**: `Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md`, `The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md`, `1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md`, `A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

Willi Münzenberg argues that Nazi propaganda is not an invincible force (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). Its power depends on the absence of a unified and aggressive counter-offensive (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). Thomas Mann summarized this necessity in May 1937, stating that the forces of good must learn to fight back rather than leaving the offensive to the powers of evil (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

## Historical Examples of Success

Münzenberg details two major historical campaigns where counter-propaganda successfully checkmated fascist movements:

### 1. The 1937 Brussels Election
In the Brussels election of April 1937, the democratic Prime Minister Paul van Zeeland faced Leon Degrelle, leader of the fascist Rexist party, which copied Nazi methods (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). The democratic coalition defeated Degrelle by executing a superior counter-campaign:
- **Humor and Satire**: They published parody newspapers (like *La Voix de Berlin* exposing Degrelle's links to Germany) and paraded donkeys and goats carrying signs reading "I vote for Degrelle because I am an ass" (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Slogans**: Simple and powerful slogans like "Rex is war" and "Votez belge!" (Vote Belgian!) were deployed (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).
- **Physical Resistance**: They blocked Rexist attempts to use violence, showing that a counter-campaign must actively prevent the fascists from monopolizing physical force (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md). Van Zeeland won with 76% of the vote (source: Propaganda_als_Waffe_9783752659368.md).

### 2. The Bombing of Guernica (Spanish Civil War)
During the Spanish Civil War, on Monday, April 26, 1937, the German Condor Legion carpet-bombed the historic Basque city of Guernica for two and a half hours, using high explosives and white phosphorus incendiaries (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md). While Basque government estimates placed casualties at 1,654, modern research indicates between 200 and 300 died (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md). 

Franco and Hitler's propaganda immediately fabricated a counter-narrative, claiming the "red hordes" and Asturian miners burned the city with gasoline before fleeing (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md). This lie was fully endorsed by the Spanish Catholic Church (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md).

However, a swift international counter-offensive mobilized world opinion, presenting documented facts and eyewitness testimonies:
- **Independent Journalism**: George Steer's reports in *The Times* and *New York Times* exposed the Condor Legion's responsibility to the world (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md).
- **Cultural Mobilization**: Pablo Picasso displayed his famous mural *Guernica* in the Republic's pavilion at the 1937 Paris International Arts Exhibition, creating a powerful, lasting symbol of fascist brutality (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md).

## Media Censorship and Control

Beevor highlights that the battle for public opinion was heavily distorted by censorship and editorial self-censorship:
- **Editorial Self-Censorship**: Dawson, the editor of *The Times*, actively blocked some of Steer's reports from the Basque country to avoid hurting German susceptibilities and damaging diplomatic relations (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md). Similarly, the right-wing *Paris Soir* suppressed reports on child massacres by correspondent Louis Delaprée (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md).
- **State Control**: The Nationalist zone enforced absolute silence on their actions, banning the Basque flag (*ikurriña*) and language, and using slogans like "If you are Spanish, speak Spanish" (source: The_battle_for_Spain_-_the_Spanish_Civil_War,_1936-1939_9780143037651.md).

## Case Study: The 1929 Sino-Soviet War

The [[sino-soviet-war-1929|1929 Sino-Soviet War]] over the [[chinese-eastern-railroad|Chinese Eastern Railroad]] demonstrates how propaganda served as a critical political-military tool (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md):
- **Early Radio Propaganda**: The Soviets deployed Khabarovsk radio broadcasts targeting Harbin, representing an early historical use of radio to spread panic and bypass Chinese news controls (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md).
- **The "White Russian" Boogeyman**: To evade the constraints of the Kellogg-Briand Pact, Moscow manufactured claims of "White Russian terrorist" border raids, establishing a false narrative of victimhood to justify its military offensive (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md).
- **CCP Loyalty Campaigns**: The Chinese Communist Party (with Liu Shao-chi leading Manchurian operations) distributed pro-Soviet pamphlets. Although deeply unpopular with the patriotic Chinese public, these campaigns successfully proved the CCP's absolute loyalty to Stalin, securing vital Soviet support for the Chinese revolution (source: 1929_Sino-Soviet_War_-_The_War_Nobody_Knew_9780700623761.md).

### 4. State-Subsidized Theater (Soviet Union)
During the early Cold War, the Soviet government subsidized massive theatrical productions to shape public opinion:
- **The Russian Question**: Simonov's highly successful play *The Russian Question* presented a stylized and propagandistic caricature of American journalists and publishers (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md). Because the production was heavily subsidized and widely promoted, its distorted depiction of American media was accepted as absolute truth by Soviet audiences (source: A_Russian_journal_9780141180199.md).
