# The Secret King

**Summary**: Stephen E. Flowers and Michael Moynihan's study of Karl Maria Wiligut (known as "Weisthor" or "Himmler's Lord of the Runes"), examining his runology, his personal influence over SS chief Heinrich Himmler, and the division between the myth and reality of Nazi occultism.

**Sources**: `The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

*The Secret King: The Myth and Reality of Nazi Occultism* provides a biographical and critical analysis of [[karl-maria-willigut]], an Austrian occultist who became the primary designer of the SS's neo-pagan rituals, symbolism, and headquarters (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

## Key Themes and Findings

### Wiligut's Role on Himmler's Personal Staff
Wiligut (known in the SS under the alias Karl Maria Weisthor, and runic name Lobesam) claimed to possess ancestral memory and a direct lineage to the Norse god Thor (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). In 1933, he was recruited by SS leader [[heinrich-himmler]] and placed on his personal staff (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). 
- **Separation from the Ahnenerbe**: Wiligut's position was separate from the *Ahnenerbe* (the SS ancestral research bureau established in 1935) (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). The Ahnenerbe operated as a larger institution subject to academic standards, whereas Wiligut worked privately and directly for Himmler, feeding him reports on esotericism, runology, and cosmology (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

### Conceptualization of Wewelsburg Castle
Wiligut heavily influenced Himmler to establish [[wewelsburg-castle]] as the global headquarters and ideological center (Order Castle or *Ordensburg*) of the SS (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). Wiligut conceptualized the castle as the "center of the world," mapping out the northern tower as a ceremonial space:
- The lowest level (the vault) served as the "Walhalla" (Hall of the Slain) to store the death's head rings of fallen SS members (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- The colonnade chamber above it featured a marble floor emblem comprised of 12 radial Sig runes (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).

### Design of the SS-Totenkopfring
Wiligut is widely credited with designing the SS death's head ring ([[ss-totenkopfring]]) (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md). The ring integrated runic characters that Himmler and Wiligut used to construct a neo-pagan SS theology:
- **Death's Head**: A call to be prepared to sacrifice one's life for the collective (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Sig Runes**: Symbolized the Schutzstaffel (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Swastika and Hagall Rune**: Kept focus on faith in National Socialist victory (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Oak Leaves**: Celebrated traditional Germanic imagery (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
- **Rules of Issuance**: The ring could not be sold or transferred. Upon a member's death or departure from the SS, the ring had to be returned to the Reichsführer-SS (source: The_Secret_King_-_The_Myth_and_Reality_of_Nazi_Occultism_9781932595253.md).
