# Angolan Civil War

**Summary**: The civil war that broke out in 1975 following Angola's decolonization from Portugal, transforming into a major Cold War proxy conflict involving direct military interventions by Cuba, South Africa, the Soviet Union, and the United States.

**Sources**: `Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md`, `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The Angolan Civil War erupted after the April 1974 Carnation Revolution in Portugal, which collapsed the Portuguese colonial empire (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). Despite the Alvor Agreement of January 1975, which attempted to establish a transitional coalition government, three rival liberation movements clashed for dominance (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Factions and Superpower Backing

- **MPLA (People's Movement for the Liberation of Angola)**: Led by Agostinho Neto, the MPLA was a Marxist-leaning movement (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). It was backed by the Soviet Union and received substantial military support from Cuba (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- **FNLA (National Front for the Liberation of Angola)**: Led by Holden Roberto, the FNLA was supported by Zaire (Congo), the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency, and China (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- **UNITA (National Union for the Total Independence of Angola)**: Led by Jonas Savimbi, UNITA initially relied on regional alliances (Zambia) and later collaborated extensively with the apartheid regime of South Africa and the United States (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## International Interventions

### 1. United States: Operation IAFEATURE
Following the fall of Saigon, U.S. Secretary of State Henry Kissinger sought to demonstrate American resolve in the Third World (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). In July 1975, the Ford administration authorized the CIA to launch Operation IAFEATURE, providing $25 million in covert military aid to the FNLA and UNITA (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 2. Soviet Union and Cuba: Operation Carlota
The Soviet Union supplied weapons and advisors to the MPLA to ensure it became the dominant partner in the new state (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). In late 1975, Cuba launched a massive independent military deployment (Operation Carlota), sending combat troops to defend Neto's government in Luanda against a joint South African and FNLA-UNITA offensive (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). This decisive intervention successfully repelled South African forces and secured MPLA control over the capital (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

### 3. South Africa
The South African apartheid regime launched a military invasion into southern Angola in 1975 to prevent the establishment of a hostile Marxist regime on its border (with Namibia) and to safeguard UNITA's positions (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Consequence and Legacy
The conflict in Angola ended hopes of superpower *détente* in the mid-1970s, as Washington viewed the Soviet-Cuban intervention as an aggressive expansion of the **[[peripheral-empire]]** (source: The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md). The war devastated Angola's infrastructure and locked the country into a multi-decade civil conflict (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
