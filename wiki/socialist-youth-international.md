# Socialist Youth International

**Summary**: The international youth organization led by Willi Münzenberg in Zurich during World War I, which became the largest and most active anti-war socialist network and the basis for the Young Communist International.

**Sources**: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md, The_Red_Millionaire_-_A_Political_Biography_of_Willy_Münzenberg,_Moscow's_Secret_Propaganda_Tsar_in_the_West,_1917-1940_9780300098471.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

The **Socialist Youth International** played a foundational role in the anti-war movement during the First World War. Established originally in Stuttgart in 1907, its center of gravity shifted to Zurich, Switzerland, under the leadership of Willi Münzenberg, who served as its president and secretary (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).

## Anti-War Organizing in Zurich

- **Autonomous Vanguard**: Frustrated by the pro-war stance and reformism of the older Social Democratic leaders (particularly in the German SPD), Münzenberg pushed for an autonomous youth section. This network became the most effective anti-war socialist grouping during WWI (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).
- **The Zimmerwald Left**: The youth international aligned closely with the Zimmerwald anti-war movement and the radical Zimmerwald Left led by Vladimir Lenin, Grigory Zinoviev, Karl Radek, and Leon Trotsky. Lenin collaborated directly with the young activists, developing their political strategy (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).
- **Jugend-Internationale**: The organization published the radical anti-war newspaper *Jugend-Internationale* ("Youth International"), which printed anti-war manifestos, including Lenin's letter to Swiss workers warning against imperialist militarism (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).
- **Transition to the Comintern**: The Socialist Youth International served as the direct precursor to the **Young Communist International**, founded in Berlin in 1919. By 1920, the organization had 49 affiliated youth groups representing 800,000 members worldwide, providing the core organizational infrastructure on which the [[comintern]] and several national communist parties were built (source: Willi_Münzenberg_-_Fighter_Against_Fascism_and_Stalinism_9780367344726.md).
