# Ethiopian Revolution

**Summary**: The 1974 revolution in Ethiopia that overthrew Emperor Haile Selassie and brought the Derg—a military junta led by Mengistu Haile Mariam—to power, establishing a Marxist-Leninist state allied with the Soviet Union.

**Sources**: `Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The Ethiopian Revolution began in 1974 amid widespread social unrest, famine, and dissatisfaction with the imperial regime of Emperor Haile Selassie (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). A committee of radical military officers, known as the Derg, seized power and declared Ethiopia a socialist state (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Mengistu and the Consolidation of Power
By 1977, Colonel Mengistu Haile Mariam emerged as the undisputed leader of the Derg by purging internal rivals (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). To consolidate control, the regime launched the "Red Terror" (Qey Shibir), a campaign of mass arrest and execution targeting urban political opponents, primarily the Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Party (EPRP) (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## Socialist Policies and Agricultural Failure
The Derg attempted a rapid social and economic transformation of Ethiopia modeled on Soviet planning (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md):
- **Land Reform**: All rural and urban land was nationalized, and peasant associations were formed to oversee collective farming (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- **Famine of 1984**: The enforcement of intensive collective farming and price controls, combined with deforestation and drought, led to agricultural collapse (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md). In Wollo province alone, between 500,000 and 750,000 people died in a famine that relief officials stated was exacerbated by the government's policies (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

## External and Internal Wars
Mengistu's government faced severe military challenges:
- **Eritrean Conflict**: A long-running war against Marxist separatist movements in Eritrea, primarily the Eritrean People's Liberation Front (EPLF) (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
- **The Ogaden War**: A 1977–1978 invasion by Somalia seeking to capture the Ogaden region (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).

The crisis prompted a full realignment of the Horn of Africa, as Ethiopia broke its traditional ties with the United States and formed a close alliance with the Soviet Union, which provided over $1 billion in military equipment and helped coordinate Cuban combat troops to save the regime (source: Global_Cold_War_-_Third_World_Interventions_and_the_Making_of_Our_Times_9780511817991.md).
