# [[ware-group|Ware Group]]

**Summary**: A secret Communist underground organization in Washington D.C. founded in 1933 by Harold Ware, consisting of federal employees who used their positions to influence policy and recruit for Soviet espionage.

**Sources**: `Witness_9780895267894.md`, `The_rise_and_fall_of_the_Soviet_Empire_9780761520573.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The **[[ware-group|Ware Group]]** was an underground apparatus of the Communist Party USA (CPUSA) operating in Washington D.C. during the 1930s (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). It was organized in 1933 by Harold Ware (1889–1935), a prominent American Communist agricultural expert and the son of "Mother" Ella Reeve Bloor (source: Witness_9780895267894.md).

## Operations and Infiltration
The group was composed of highly educated, college-trained New Deal bureaucrats who had entered government service during the [[great-depression|Great Depression]] (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). Members paid party dues and operated in cells to ensure operational security (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). 

The group had some sixty or seventy members infiltrating key government agencies (source: Witness_9780895267894.md):
- **Agricultural Adjustment Administration (AAA)** and **Department of Agriculture**: The primary initial target, where Harold Ware, [[alger-hiss|Alger Hiss]], John Abt, Lee Pressman, and Nathan Witt were employed.
- **Department of Justice**
- **National Labor Relations Board (NLRB)**
- **Treasury Department**

## Transition to Espionage
Under the coordination of CPUSA underground chief [[j-peters|J. Peters]], the [[ware-group|Ware Group]] served as a highly effective recruiting ground for Soviet military intelligence (GRU) (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). Peters and courier [[whittaker-chambers|Whittaker Chambers]] selected the most promising and highly placed members of the [[ware-group|Ware Group]], separating them from the general party cells to work in active espionage (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). [[alger-hiss|Alger Hiss]] was one of the key recruits transitioned from the [[ware-group|Ware Group]] into the active GRU military apparatus (source: Witness_9780895267894.md).

## Exposure
The existence of the [[ware-group|Ware Group]] was first revealed to the public by [[whittaker-chambers|Chambers]] during his testimony before the House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC) in August 1948 (source: Witness_9780895267894.md). In 1951, former member Lee Pressman confirmed the existence of the group under oath, naming Harold Ware, John Abt, Nathan Witt, and himself as members (source: Witness_9780895267894.md).
