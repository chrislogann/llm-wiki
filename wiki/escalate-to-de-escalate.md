# Escalate to De-escalate

**Summary**: A controversial military doctrine attributed to the Russian Federation involving the threat of or actual first-use of limited tactical nuclear weapons in a conventional conflict to compel an adversary to halt hostilities on terms favorable to Moscow.

**Sources**: `Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The doctrine of **"escalate to de-escalate"** represents a critical component of Russia's security strategy, particularly in its planning for conflicts against conventional powers like NATO (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

## Core Logic of the Doctrine
The strategy is designed to offset the conventional superiority of the United States and its allies (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). Under this doctrine, if Russia finds itself in a conventional conflict that threatens its national interests, it reserves the right to employ or threaten a limited, low-yield tactical nuclear strike (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

The goal of this escalation is not to initiate a global thermonuclear exchange, but to shock the opponent (specifically the U.S. and NATO) into recognizing that continuing the war risks a massive nuclear escalation (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). This is intended to force the adversary to "de-escalate"—halting their military operations and accepting a peace settlement on Russia's terms (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).

## Threat Projection
In *Putin's Playbook*, Rebekah Koffler explains that the threat of nuclear use is a primary tool of political leverage for Vladimir Putin (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md). By routinely emphasizing Russia's nuclear readiness and deploying dual-use delivery systems (capable of carrying conventional or nuclear warheads), the Kremlin projects a high willingness to take risks, exploiting the democratic public's aversion to nuclear war to achieve diplomatic and geopolitical concessions (source: Putin's_Playbook_-_Russia's_Secret_Plan_to_Defeat_America_9781684510030.md).
