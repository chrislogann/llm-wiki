# In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft

**Summary**: A contemporary report on the rising popularity of witchcraft, magical services, and protective amulets in Russia, serving as a psychological defense mechanism during a period of ongoing military conflict and economic uncertainty.

**Sources**: `In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-18

---

The ongoing conflict in Ukraine and the resulting economic stress have triggered a significant resurgence of mysticism and occult practices in contemporary Russia (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md). This trend reflects a historical pattern of mysticism rising during periods of intense national anxiety.

## Rising Popularity and Psychological Defense
State pollster VTsIOM reported that 85% of Russians have dabbled in magical practices, with nearly half of the population believing in future prediction or magical powers—a significant increase from less than a third in 2019 (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md). VTsIOM characterizes this turn to belief as a "tool of psychological defense" against military threats and geopolitical challenges (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).

## Soldiers and Magic
A new demographic of clients has emerged, consisting of soldiers fighting on the front lines in eastern Ukraine (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md). Witches such as Natalia Malinovskaya offer protection spells and love spells (sought by soldiers concerned about partner fidelity) during their leaves (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).

## Consumer Demand for Occult Goods
Retail sales data from ATOL indicates that consumer demand for protective amulets and crystal balls has more than doubled, while demand for protective aspen stakes has quadrupled (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md). Popular items include:
* **Black Obsidian Spheres**: Sourced from volcanic glass, these are highly sought after as a stone of safety (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).
* **Tarot Readings**: Venues like the voodoo-themed bar *Marie Laveau* have grown in popularity by matching the cultural moment (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).

## State and Orthodox Church Backlash
The revival of occultism has met with institutional resistance:
* **Legislative Action**: Lawmakers have submitted a bill to ban advertisements for services like astrology and energy healing to protect vulnerable citizens from financial exploitation (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).
* **Orthodox Church Position**: Patriarch Kirill of the Russian Orthodox Church decried the "mass manipulative influence" of psychics and fortune-tellers, defining fortune-telling as a "devilish power" associated with "satanism" (source: In tough times, Russians are turning to witchcraft.md).
