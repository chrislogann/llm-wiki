# Dictatorship of Capital

**Summary**: The definition of fascism as the open or disguised dictatorship of monopoly-finance capital over society, emerging from the inherent contradictions of capitalist accumulation and the subordination of labor.

**Sources**: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md

**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

---

The concept of fascism as the **dictatorship of capital** is a central thesis of Michael Joseph Roberto's analysis of interwar American history. Rather than defining fascism by secondary European features—such as paramilitary mass movements, charismatic leadership (a Führer or Duce), or open illiberalism—this Marxist perspective views fascism as a direct function of monopoly-finance capitalism (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).

## Core Arguments

- **Finance Capital as the Fountainhead**: Following communist writers A. B. Magil and Henry Stevens (*The Peril of Fascism*, 1938), this view posits that homegrown fascism in the United States originates directly from Wall Street and Big Business. In times of crisis, the ruling capitalist class uses the power of the state to protect the institution of private profit (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Marxist Laws of Accumulation**: Drawing from Karl Marx's *Capital* and Lewis Corey's *The Decline of American Capitalism* (1934), the dictatorship of capital is seen as arising from the [[marxist-laws-of-accumulation]] (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Disguised Character**: Unlike European fascism, which was overtly illiberal, the American dictatorship of capital is highly disguised, often wrapping itself in the flag, proclaiming "anti-fascism," and marching under the banner of liberty and democracy to leave the ruling class undisturbed in power (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
- **Coercive vs. Non-Coercive Coordination**: Totalitarian rule is achieved not only through a "terroristic political coordination" (such as industrial espionage, strike-breaking, and state repression) but also through "non-terroristic economic-technical coordination" that operates by manipulating consumer needs and public consciousness (source: Coming_of_the_American_Behemoth_-_The_Origins_of_Fascism_in_the_United_States,_1920_-1940_9781583677315.md).
