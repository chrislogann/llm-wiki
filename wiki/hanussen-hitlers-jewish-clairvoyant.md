# Hitler's Jewish Clairvoyant

**Summary**: Mel Gordon's biography of Erik Jan Hanussen, detailing his rise as a prominent Weimar-era clairvoyant, his relationships with Nazi leadership, and the conspiracy surrounding his Reichstag fire prediction and subsequent murder.

**Sources**: `Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

*Hitler's Jewish Clairvoyant* by Mel Gordon is the first English-language biography of Herschmann Chaim Steinschneider, who performed under the stage name [[erik-jan-hanussen]]. The book chronicles his stage career as a hypnotist, mind-reader, and psychic detective, as well as his close, complex relationship with high-ranking members of the National Socialist German Workers' Party (NSDAP) (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

## Key Themes and Findings

### Identity and Rise to Prominence
Hanussen was born in Vienna to a poor Jewish actor but hid his Jewish ancestry to succeed in the increasingly nationalistic and anti-Semitic environments of post-WWI Austria and Germany (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). He established himself in Berlin in the early 1930s as a high-society clairvoyant and publisher of occult tabloids like the *Bunte Wochenschau* (B/V) (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

### Relationship with Adolf Hitler
According to historical evidence and testimonies from figures like Otto Strasser (brother of Gregor Strasser), Hanussen met Adolf Hitler in June or July 1932 at the Hotel Kaiserhof, Hitler's Berlin command post (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). During the summer of 1932:
- Hanussen provided Hitler with regular, private lessons in public speaking, body language, mass psychology, and the staging of meetings for dramatic effect (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).
- Hanussen proposed the creation of a "University of the Occult" to help anchor the future *Führerstaat*, an idea to which Hitler agreed (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).
- Following their meeting, Hanussen redesigned the layout of his tabloid to use Fraktur print (the archaic Gothic script favored by Hitler) and shifted its editorial focus to promote Hitler and National Socialism (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).

### The Reichstag Fire Arson and Murder
Hanussen's famous public "prediction" of the [[reichstagsbrand|Reichstag Fire]] on February 27, 1933, was actually based on insider information (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). He was close associates with Wolf-Heinrich Graf von Helldorff, a Berlin SA leader who was privy to the Nazi plan to burn the building (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md). 
Because Hanussen knew too much about SA involvement and held substantial financial leverage over Helldorff and other SA officers, he was kidnapped and assassinated by the SA in late March 1933 (source: Hanussen_-_Hitler's_Jewish_Clairvoyant_9780922915682.md).
