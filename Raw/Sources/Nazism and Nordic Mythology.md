# Norse Mythology and Nazi Propaganda

[Original article](https://gns.wisc.edu/2017/09/06/norse-mythology-and-nazi-propaganda/)

This essay argues that Nazi propaganda misappropriated Norse mythology and Viking symbols to legitimize militarism, racial ideology, and expansion. The source surveys posters and symbols to show how those associations distort the historical meanings of the myths and contribute to stigma around their study.
