# Nazi Misappropriation of Norse Mythology

A clear pattern in the source is the use of Norse imagery as political branding rather than as historical interpretation. The article frames this as misappropriation: the Nazis attached selective symbols, ships, and mythic motifs to a violent modern ideology while ignoring the myths’ original meanings.
