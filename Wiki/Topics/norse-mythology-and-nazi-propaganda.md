# Norse Mythology and Nazi Propaganda

The source links Norse mythology, Viking symbols, and Nazi propaganda through repeated visual themes: SS imagery, ships, swastikas, runes, winter landscapes, and Ragnarok references. The compiled note should be read as a study of propaganda technique and historical distortion, not as a validation of the ideology it describes.
